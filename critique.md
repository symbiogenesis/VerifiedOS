# Critique: Remaining Gaps in the Architecture and Specification

*Non-normative. A gap catalogue for the current state of [verification-maximal-os.md](verification-maximal-os.md) and its companions, checked against the artifacts: the atomic-requirements register and the four derived views. Where an entry here and the register disagree, the register wins and this document is defective.*

The review machinery makes the project's real position **countable**, and it is worse than the prose reads. Twenty-two crown-jewel specifications are named; **two** are authored. Seven theorem targets are named; **none** can start, because each is premised on a specification that does not exist. Every hedge the design deleted was deleted on the strength of a verified primary, and **no primary is verified**.

**Not one of the findings below is a legibility problem.** The two headline gaps this catalogue has watched close were both closed by writing a paragraph, and no paragraph closes any of these.

## The dominant fact, which is not a finding

**Every hedge is spent, no primary is verified, and the interval between is claimed at the tier it holds.** This is not an entry, because it is not a defect: it is stated where it belongs. It sits ahead of the findings because it remains the single most important true thing about the project's assurance position, and because five of the findings below are read against it.

The *verify rather than hedge* rule is applied consistently and its logic is sound at the fixed point: a mechanism whose only job is to catch the failure of a formally verified primary buys little against the cost of a second modeled mechanism. On that rule the design deletes the MMU, PMP, the S/U rings, the IOMMU, the initialization-tag plane, MTE, shadow stacks, the Harvard split, and every disjoint backstop on the machine (R-17-037, R-17-045). §17 states the hedge that remains: "the **formal verification of CHERI itself**," resting on "the **RTL ⊑ Sail** arrow … and its Coq-native restatement for the CHERI-RISC-V profile as the residual." That arrow is the layer the same document calls **the least-built layer of the stack** (R-17-039, R-18-010). The CHERI-TAL soundness metatheorem that replaces the initialization-tag plane is theorem target `CJ-TAL-SOUND`, premised on the ISA profile and **not started**. The certifying compiler, the on-device checker, the Cerise universal contract, the secure-compilation theorem: none exists.

So the rule is sound at the fixed point and its premise is false everywhere before it. On the entire trajectory to that fixed point (a multi-year interval on this roster, and the only state the project has ever occupied) the primaries are *unverified*, a hedge against an unverified primary is exactly ordinary defense in depth, and any device that executes in that interval runs with the elegance and without the argument. A single CHERI logic error in monotonicity, revocation, or the tag plane fails every isolation boundary on the machine at once, with nothing beneath it and, until `CJ-TAL-SOUND` and the Coq refinement close, no proof above it.

**The interim claim is the knob that turns, and it is the only one that can.** Demanding retained backstops asks for the wrong thing: MMU, PMP, the S/U rings, the IOMMU, MTE, and the Harvard split are **silicon**, so "retain until the theorem closes" means fabricating them, modelling them in Sail, and paying the second-modeled-mechanism cost the rule exists to price, and R-18-002 forecloses a capability-degraded interim by name. §1 carries the discharge tier on G2 itself (R-01-002a, R-01-002b): the software premise admits no interval, because nothing runs uncertified and a missing certificate costs delivery rather than admission; the hardware premise stands at **evidence tier** (rvfi, Sail-generated SystemVerilog under commercial FEV, the imported-core netlist audit) rather than **theorem tier** (the Kami/Kôika Coq refinement), every FPGA and early-silicon unit asserts G2 at that tier and no higher, the tier rises per core when that core's unbounded refinement is admitted and never at an intermediate rung, and an attestation or datasheet asserting G2 without its tier is a review-gate finding. §3 carries the inheritance (R-03-006). The retirement condition is R-18-010's rungs, on R-05-022's two-list discipline.

**What the tier statement buys is legibility, not safety, and the exposure is untouched.** The interval is multi-year and the tier is the weaker one for all of it; what a reader of §1 and §3 alone learns is that this is so. Two consequences run into the findings below. Whether a future datasheet honours R-01-002b is a semantic question `check.ps1` cannot reach, which is finding 2. And the tier claim is load-bearing for exactly as long as the interval lasts, which makes it the cheapest thing in this catalogue to have written and the easiest to let go stale.

## What is not built

**1. Nothing load-bearing is built, and the specification is precise enough to say how little.** [crown-jewels.md](crown-jewels.md) is the instrument that says so, and its status column reads: of twenty-two crown-jewel specifications, **two are authored** (the frozen ISA profile and the apex theorem's machine-checked statement), three are partial, **seventeen are not authored**. The seven theorem targets each depend on a row above; seventeen of those premises do not exist, so most of the proof program cannot begin rather than merely being unfinished.

The repository's own history sharpens this. As of 2026-08-17: forty-three days, 407 commits, roughly 2.5 MB of design documents, and **zero lines of system code**: the non-Markdown paths `git ls-files` returns are the consistency checker and the apex-theorem statement artifact with its assumption gate, and nothing yet compiles to the machine. Those figures are a hand-copied snapshot rather than a recomputed count, which is the drift the register's own discipline (R-05-151a, and every count `check.ps1` rebuilds) exists to prevent, reappearing in its critic. Read them as a snapshot with a direction, and recompute before citing.

The project's only demonstrated velocity is in producing obligations. That is a defensible way to spend an early phase, and §18 and R-01-003 are candid that the as-existing assurance is low. What is *not* stated anywhere is the ratio's direction: the obligation count grows monotonically and the discharge count is one. Every hardening that makes the design safer adds a crown jewel, and nothing in the process retires one except authoring it, which is why the inventory's real contribution is spent only if the status column is run as a **burn-down with a stated direction** rather than as a census that can only grow.

## The review gate audits its own reflection

**2. The gate is referential and the risk is semantic.** §5's release gate exists because "a proof against a wrong spec verifies perfectly," and R-18-034 states plainly that the gate "audits the register rather than this prose," so an unverified extraction stands between the reviewer and the design of record and is declared the authoritative object. That an automated checker cannot decide whether a statement is the *right* statement is a property of checkers and not a defect anyone can repair, and charging it as a gap is a category error. What is chargeable is the residue, and there are exactly three pieces of it.

**The instrument is strong and is the wrong shape, which is a fact to build on rather than to complain about.** `check.ps1` checks **referential integrity**: that cited bookmarks resolve, that requirements carry traces, that no view drops a requirement in its bearing subsections, that no asserted count disagrees with its artifact, that no document carries encoding damage. Its reach includes this document: every count asserted here is anchored in the checker's registry and recomputed, so an edition of this critique cannot quietly disagree with the artifacts about a number, and the figures in finding 1 are stale only because no claim holds them. A requirement can nonetheless resolve perfectly, trace perfectly, appear in every view, count correctly, and state the wrong thing.

**Face one: the review budget is spent partly on the document rather than on the machine, and it wants a tag.** A class of acceptance criteria is discharged by inspecting the prose rather than the artifact: *"the axiom is stated in §6 and §17, not implied"* (R-06-014); *"the decision is stated as a design choice with its consequence, not omitted"* (R-10-011); *"the consent TCB is named in §6 rather than hidden in userland"* (R-06-018); *"the split is stated rather than blurred"* (R-13-023); *"the cost is booked rather than omitted"* (R-12-020); *"the trade is stated in §15, §12, and here"* (R-17-051). The size of the class depends entirely on where the line is drawn, and no single number is defensible. Matching only criteria of the quoted shape (the test being that the prose says a thing, in those words) yields **seven of 1192 acceptance criteria**. Widening to any criterion whose test uses *stated*, *booked*, *named*, *recorded*, or *declared* without naming an artifact or `check.ps1` yields **116**; narrowing that to the ones citing a `§` as the thing to open yields **25**. A figure quoted without its rule is the same defect as the one being charged, so the honest statement is the band and the method: **7 strictly, 25 on the reading most people mean, 116 at the loosest**, each reproducible against the register, all three a snapshot and a lower bound, since amendments keep adding entries of exactly this class. These entries are **not** defects under R-05-153: that rule catches a claim that cannot be restated as an atomic, testable requirement, and "the consent TCB is named in §6" is atomic and is testable by inspection, which is a test. The defect is one layer over, in R-05-152 making the register the audited object: a requirement whose acceptance criterion is a fact about the commentary spends the gate's scarcest resource, a reviewer's attention on the one pass that decides whether the specification says the right thing, on editorial hygiene. The repair is a **tag rather than a defect entry**: mark the spec-hygiene entries as such, so the review can price them instead of discovering them one at a time.

**The amendment rate is the same finding from the other side.** Four gaps this catalogue named landed within twenty-four hours of being written, and the coverage matrix inside a day of the one after. That responsiveness is the strongest evidence in the repository that the specification workstream is real, and it is also the problem: a corpus that can be amended this fast is a corpus whose amendments outrun any review that reads them.

**Face two: the extraction-defect list opens on the wrong axis, and its own stated class is empty.** The list is empty, and the register is right that "an empty list is not the same as a swept one." But look at what the list is instrumented to catch. Its findings arrive from building a derived view and machine-checking it, which reports an enumeration two documents disagree about (the surviving CSRs [isa-profile.md](isa-profile.md) §5 has to name are the standing example), never a passage that would not decompose, because nothing re-reads the prose against that question. **R-05-153's actual class, a normative claim that would not decompose, has zero entries**: not one of the 1192 requirements has yet been booked as standing for a passage that resisted restatement, and face one's entries are not the missing ones, because they decomposed fine, into requirements about the document. So the two readings stand. Either the prose was already atomic throughout (implausible for a document whose §17 entries routinely run five clauses deep with three parenthetical qualifications) or the extraction **accommodated** the prose where it should have confronted it. The register does not claim otherwise: its standing instruction is that sweep 2's question "has not been asked exhaustively, so further instances should be assumed present rather than absent." The channel that reports what the *prose* refused to decompose is not running, and an agenda drawn from enumerations alone means the meeting R-18-035 calls the review gate's agenda has barely started.

**Face three: the derived views can be wrong in the half nothing checks, and there are more of them every month.** [crown-jewels.md](crown-jewels.md) is declared "Normative as a **view**." The *quantitative* half of that class is closed the only way such things close: `check.ps1` parses the status column into its three classes, recomputes the ratio, and fails where a document asserts a figure the artifact does not bear. What is untouched is the qualitative half, which is most of it. Whether a row's status is *honest* (that `partial` is not doing work `not authored` should do), whether a cited requirement is the *right* ground, whether a discharge mode in [coverage-matrix.md](coverage-matrix.md) reports what its cited requirements actually carry: invisible by construction. Every artifact that arrives widens that surface, each one machine-checked for **presence** and none for **truth**, and a view is normative whether or not it has been read. The only lever this finding has is substitution: *whether R-17-030a's seams are the right ones* is not answerable, but it is **replaced** by a mechanical question it implies, whether every requirement using the vocabulary of refusal is conferred or dispositioned (finding 3). That substitution is available wherever a qualitative claim has a lexical shadow it can be held against, and it is not available for most of the list above.

**3. A membership claim is decidable against its own members and undecidable against the world.** R-17-016 closes the crown-jewel list by conferral: membership is asserted by each requirement that has it, [crown-jewels.md](crown-jewels.md) is the one place the conferrals are collected, and `check.ps1` fails on a conferral with no row or a row with no requirement. R-17-030r and R-10-013a carry the identical rule: a requirement specifying a mechanism whose failure action is to stop records the refusal against itself on a `· Fail-closed:` property line, a requirement placing state under the monotonic counter records it on a `· RoT-fresh:` line, the R-17-030 seams and R-10-013 collect them, and the checker fails on a conferral no collection carries and on a seam standing under no conferral. Twenty-one conferrals against fifteen seams, four against one enumeration, both directions decidable. What no checker can grep for is not membership; it is the *predicate*, and conferral does not need to.

**Conferral closes completeness for none of the three sets, and the registers say so.** A specification that ought to confer crown-jewel status and does not is exactly as invisible as a refusal that ought to confer and does not; what conferral closes is the *collection's disagreement with the requirements*, which is the smaller property. Each register claims the decidable half in its own text and declines the other, which is the only honest form of the claim.

**The machinery is not the result, and a completeness convention fails exactly where it is most expensive.** Against the completeness residue the checker carries the only instrument available to it: the vocabulary of refusal is over-approximated across every requirement body, and each entry it catches must confer, be collected, or be dispositioned by name with a reason. Run against a register believed complete, it returns four members that register did not carry, and one of them is the sharpest member in it. **Every fault detector on this machine answers detection by stopping** (uncorrectable ECC, tag integrity, the control-flow signature on the three protected sequences, the sentinel's lockstep comparator, residual synchronizer failure), each of them right to stop, and the adversary who provokes them is the radiated-EMI and electromagnetic-fault-injection adversary §3 admits by name and §15 shields against. A probe held near the enclosure stops a device that is behaving correctly, needs no software access at all, and gives R-17-030l's life-safety composition a fourth cause: a user who cannot place an emergency call because a detector caught an injected fault. The other three are the entropy health test, whose sole failure action is fail-stop and whose meeting is every operation needing fresh randomness; the display path, whose visible blank is a caught fail-stop on the surface the consent prompt is drawn on, reaching denial of the prompt from a direction the compositor seam does not cover; and budget admission refusal, which also costs R-17-030e its claim to be the only member whose denial cannot reach a running unit.

**What stands is the residue.** The vocabulary agenda is lexical, proves no totality, and is defeated by an author who rewords rather than decides. The dispositions are judgments recorded in a tool. Neither is a quantifier over the semantic predicate, because there is none to build. So the only thing worth building is the agenda that forces a person to decide, on every run, for every candidate the vocabulary can reach. Twenty-seven candidates were dispositioned against the current registers. The fourteen on the RoT-fresh side all resolved the same way, that they enforce the floor or provide the counter and place no state under it, which is the only evidence in this repository that an enumeration closed by convention was right.

## Deletion has no opposing artifact

**4. The first release has no stated minimum viable capability, so deletion is unfalsifiable.** Assemble what §18 commits to for the first parts: **no browser**, deferred as the largest porting program; **1–2 GB** of planar single-tier SRAM; six C-class cores with software rendering at initial bring-up; no JIT anywhere; no fixed-function GPU; a few-active-things machine where the foreground is fast and the background is "very nearly stopped."

Nowhere does the specification state what this device **does for a user**. There is no requirement naming a target workload the first release must serve, no floor on the roster, no acceptance criterion of the form *this configuration runs this set of things at this quality*. [userspace-porting.md](userspace-porting.md) is the closest thing and declines the job in its own words: the applications are "the **elective** applications" and the roster is "elective and stageable," a sequencing document by design, so nothing on it can fail to arrive. [performance-estimates.md](performance-estimates.md) scores deltas against an out-of-order baseline and reports the honest matrix by archetype; it does not answer whether the resulting absolute is a product.

That omission is what makes *delete-rather-than-defend* structurally unfalsifiable. Every deletion is scored on the axis it improves (one fewer Sail obligation, one fewer block on the least-built arrow, one fewer trust anchor), and the ledger runs only that way, because the axis that would push back (does the surviving machine still do the job?) has no requirement to be violated and no artifact to run. Twenty-plus deletions are each booked as a win at specification time, and not one of them can be contradicted until silicon exists. A design method with no possible negative result is not being tested by its own process; it is being narrated by it. No register closes this one, because the missing artifact is a product floor and writing one is a decision rather than a composition.

## The theorem tier covers the hardware nobody has written

**5. Single-prover purity holds exactly where nothing exists, and is abandoned where artifacts do.** The Kami/Kôika Coq refinement is "the only unbounded vehicle" (R-17-039) and is authored **for the net-new blocks that have no legacy RTL**: the capability/tag-carrying DMA fabric, the TDM NoC, the fixed-function sequencers. Those blocks do not exist. The cores that *do* exist (CHERI-CVA6, Ara, Gemmini) close by riding "Sail-generated SystemVerilog plus commercial FEV," which R-17-039 concedes "trusts the equivalence tool," or by a deferred proof over shipped SystemVerilog conditioned on "**if** a foundational Verilog-semantics account matures."

The absence contract has the identical shape: Kôika blocks close in-prover as a predicate over the Coq term; imported cores close on "a structural **audit, not a theorem**." [absence-contract.md](absence-contract.md) states this plainly and honestly, and states that day one *is* the imported-core half, "which is exactly the half that closes on audit."

The composed statement is uncomfortable and is nowhere made: **the strongest claims this design makes about the processor it will actually run on rest on a commercial tool's say-so and a netlist search, while the Coq tier (the whole argument for the single-prover discipline) covers only hardware that has not been designed.** That is not dishonest; both halves are booked. It is unassembled, and assembled it says the trust story for the CPU is evidence-tier for the foreseeable future. The absence contract's day-one availability, which §18 celebrates, is availability of the *weaker* discharge form. R-01-002a names the evidence-tier vehicles where G2 is claimed, so the *isolation* claim carries its tier; the observation here, that the single-prover discipline's own tier covers only blocks nobody has designed, is stated in neither §17 nor the absence contract. The tier is carried by the claim that rides the hardware and not by the argument for the discipline that is supposed to underwrite it.

## Booked but open

**6. One word is doing two jobs on the consent path, and the mechanism work is finished.** §8/§12 close the mechanism side completely: robust delimited declassification, RoT secure-attention indicator, RoT-latched input ownership for the prompt's duration, a ceiling-bounded lease in place of an unevaluable *while-active*. That work is excellent and it is finished, and this entry asks for no mechanism, because the obvious mechanisms are the wrong trade. Grant rate-limiting, consent-pattern anomaly detection, and habituation heuristics are policy judgments with no specification to be proven against: each would be a new oracle whose wrongness no proof catches, added to the one path §6 keeps smallest, deciding on the user's behalf by heuristic what the design elsewhere refuses to decide without a theorem. R-17-013c books the substance honestly, recording that the ceiling is exercised *at machine rate* once an autonomous agent is the principal doing the asking, with standing grants relieving the fatigue only by widening scope, and its acceptance criterion is that "no mechanism is claimed to decide the fatigue-versus-scope trade on the user's behalf," which is the right refusal stated in the right place.

What is left is a **vocabulary defect, not a design gap**, and it is worth one entry because of what it costs a reader. R-17-013 files "the user consented to the wrong thing" as the irreducible ceiling, and that name covers two claims. *The user did not understand what they authorized* is genuinely outside any theorem and always will be. *A correctly-rendered, genuinely-attested prompt can be issued often enough, or shaped well enough, that a competent user authorizes something they would refuse on reflection* is a different claim, and it is unaddressed rather than irreducible: a repository-wide search returns nothing on grant rate-limiting, grant-pattern anomaly detection, consent habituation, or comprehension scaffolding, and the only rate-limiter in the corpus guards unlock attempts (R-09-018, R-12-017), never grants. The design defends duress credentials, biometric vaults, and seized-device forensics, a threat model that presumes a high-risk user, and social-engineering an unspoofable prompt is precisely how such users are compromised. **Split R-17-013** into the comprehension ceiling (irreducible, keep saying so) and the abuse-resistance ceiling (open, with the argument above for why no admissible mechanism closes it), so that *unspoofable* stops being quoted where *abuse-resistant* is what a reader wants.

**7. Duress books its own failure modes and not the dynamic that creates them.** R-17-054(3) books more than it is usually credited with: the countermeasure as "not a guarantee," the already-extracted-keys case, the **image-first-compel-second** case by name, and irreversibility on accidental entry. R-17-030h carries the seam, stating that the erase is irreversible on the wrong side of a human protocol this design does not model. What none of them books is the dynamic a publicly specified duress feature creates: an adversary who knows the feature exists compels *both* credentials, or watches for the erase and escalates against the person rather than the device. The mechanism's real protection is that human protocol, the document names it as unmodeled without saying what it costs when it fails, and the erase is irreversible on exactly the side where the coercion continues. The entry is narrow and it is not closed, because overclaiming this feature endangers the user who relies on it most.

**8. Physical and sub-partition side channels grow more load-bearing as the device model grows more adversarial.** Probe-level power/EM, TEMPEST coupling, RF fingerprinting against link-layer unlinkability, macro-internal and inter-tier coupling, and the event-driven sensor readout's data-dependent timing are all booked (R-17-003b, R-17-055, R-17-058/059) and none is closed. The *seized-and-once-unlocked* and *duress* cases put a physical adversary in scope **by design**, which raises the weight of exactly these residuals. R-17-059 states the sharpest version itself: "the design has **no defence-in-depth layer beneath the package boundary** on the memory path … and if it is ever judged wrong there is no second mechanism behind it." That is the dominant fact again, at the physical layer, stated by the document against itself, and unlike the isolation tier it has no ladder, because a package boundary admits no staged discharge form.

One of these is stated in the form this entry asks for, and it is the pattern the rest want: R-17-058a says the power/EM position plainly, in §17 and in the register. The crypto core carries no masking; resistance to a probing adversary is not claimed; the constant-time apparatus is architectural and says nothing about the analog observation; masking with a machine-checked probing-model proof is named as the only construction-grade answer, with its cost in randomness, area, and a leakage model that is itself an assumption; and the four things that would reverse the decision are enumerated, so the residual is falsifiable rather than perpetual. The `B-01`×`P-6` cell reads the same way, carrying the architectural half and booking the analog half instead of covering the pair with one mode. What is unchanged is the ceiling: none of the other residuals here has had that paragraph written for it, and each still reads stronger in summary than in construction.

**9. The degraded-subset rate is budgeted and no requirement names the number.** R-17-035 splits containment (fast, monotone, no proof) from remediation (proof-gated) and budgets *the window*; the quantity in which repeated forcing of the degraded subset is visible is **windows per unit time**. R-03-007/008 put denial in scope and R-17-030m supplies the quantity: the degraded-subset *rate* is budgeted, with entries into containment recorded as an attested countable event class rather than absorbed as containment succeeding, and the `B-06`×`P-5` cell reads `proved / detected`, which is the honest mode for it. What is open is what the ceiling *is*. The obligation says the quantity must exist and be recorded, no requirement names a figure, and a well-posed budget with no figure in it is materially better than an unposed one and is not the same as a closed one.

## Not gaps: the ceilings this document keeps naming

*These are stated correctly, they are permanent or nearly so, and numbering them as findings would pad this catalogue's own count in the way it charges the specification with inflating claims elsewhere.*

- **Specification error is first-ranked, and is the reason the inventory exists.** Twenty-two crown jewels, each a small oracle that must be *stated* right and whose wrongness no proof catches: the delimited-release bound, the ASN.1/UPER codec, the dedup keyed-digest interface, the HAL's DMA postconditions, the IDL initialization annotations, the WCET latency magnitudes, the `Zkt`/`Zvkt` leakage model, the Ztso and static-prediction fetch models, every wire-format mapping, each Lustre program. Unchanged in kind, large in extent, and measurable, which is the whole point of having counted them. The one actionable part of this is not a ceiling and belongs to finding 1: measure it as a burn-down.
- **Fabrication trust is evidence-based, not proof-based.** IRIS backside inspection makes fab an obtainable-evidence question rather than an act of faith, and the tier grading is a genuinely strong position: everything that *acts* is imaged, and what is not imaged cannot act. The ceiling stands where R-17-063 puts it: coarser than the smallest features, *evidence not proof*, and a sub-resolution fab adversary stays in scope. Narrowed, not removed.
- **The bedrock assumptions, the human, and the eUICC stay outside the trusted computer.** MLWE/MSIS and ECDLP/CDH are irreducible axioms; the user is outside every consent theorem (finding 6); the eUICC remains "the one tolerated foreign computer," contained to zero platform authority but an admitted exception to the design's defining claim. All three are permanent, and the *no foreign computers / everything verified* framing should keep saying so out loud.

---

*A note on what is not here: the mechanism-level architecture is settled, internally consistent, and in several places genuinely excellent. The three seam registers, the two-class entropy record, the containment/remediation split, and the graded-inspectability position are all strong work.*

*The composition axis is instrumented in both directions. [coverage-matrix.md](coverage-matrix.md) composes across **properties at a boundary**, one cell per pair, machine-checked for presence; R-17-030a composes across **mechanisms at a seam**, on the rule §17 uses for retained mechanisms and for refusals alike; and R-01-002a/R-03-006 carry the discharge tier up to where the claims are read. Each of those is paragraph-sized and engineering-free, changes nothing about the machine, and changes what a reader of §1 or §3 alone concludes.*

*The qualification is that **composition is a legibility instrument and none of the nine findings above is a legibility problem.** The product floor is a decision. And everything composed is machine-checked for presence and none of it for truth, which is finding 2 landing on the newest artifacts in the repository.*
