# The Product-Gate Contract: Absolute Limits, the Result Record, and What Rejects a Candidate

> Non-normative, governed by [R-18-004a](requirements-register.md), and a companion to [the performance estimates](performance-estimates.md).
> It decides nothing today. It states the absolute pass and fail limits a candidate is measured against on one representative workload, the record every result is written into, and the predicates that reject a report or a candidate, all authored before any candidate exists so that no limit here can have been chosen to make a particular candidate pass.
> Where this document and [the register](requirements-register.md) disagree, the register wins and this document is defective. Where it and [the estimates](performance-estimates.md) disagree, the estimates are a non-normative estimate and this document is a gate over measurements, so neither corrects the other: a limit here is stated against a row there by its id and copies no figure from it.

## How to read this

R-18-004a is a floor of eight workload members carried together on one composed configuration, each stated at a quality a candidate can miss, and R-18-004c names the gate and the artifact that decides each of its clauses. What neither states is the product question the critique puts to the plan: a candidate can meet every member, clear every security admission rule, and still be a device a user does not keep, because a correct refusal is a product failure when it lands on the work the user was doing. This document is the item Q1 of [the implementation plan](implementation-checklist.md) authors to make that failure nameable before a candidate exists: **absolute** limits, in the user's units, on response latency, sustained power and temperature, capacity refusals, update turnaround, and operation without the external composition service, over a workload wider than the floor's minimum.

Three rules shape everything below. **A limit is stated against a register clause or an estimates row by its id and never restates its figure**, so that the arithmetic under the estimates stays [the checker's compounds group's](../tools/check-rules.md) to hold and the thresholds stay the register's; the only numbers this document adds are the ones it owns, and those are collected in one declared-parameters table with a status column rather than scattered through the text. **A wall-clock clause is decided by nothing until the artifacts R-18-004c names exist**: the latency magnitudes R-17-041 books and the operating point and slot share R-11-017's artifact emits, a cycle count being no wall-clock figure until an operating point is selected. Every row of §2 therefore names which rung of that ladder decides it, and a report claiming a verdict on a rung whose instrument cannot reach it is rejected by §4 rather than read. **The numeric envelope limits are proposed and not taken.** Sustained power, temperature and update turnaround are the physical envelope R-18-004a's fifth Accept says reaches no pre-silicon gate, and Q10 reserves the arms that envelope forces to the product's owner; the values in §2's declared-parameters table bind nothing until the product's owner ratifies them, and every row carrying one says so.

What this document is not: it is not R-18-004d's versioned demonstration set, which names per member the compartments live, the population rung, the surfaces composited, the radio state, the input cadence and the pass condition, and is fixed before the R-15-108 exploration runs; §1 states members and transitions as capabilities and fixes none of those per-member facts, so it takes no register-adjacent act. It is not an instrument: no benchmark dashboard precedes a reproducible result, and the report and gate machinery already in the tree, [the freeze contract's report and gate](freeze-measurement-contract.md#7-the-report) and [the bank-count contract's inadmissibility table](bank-count-dse-contract.md#5-what-makes-a-report-inadmissible), is the shape reused here. And it carries no measurement: no candidate image exists, so every verdict this document defines is unreached, and the item's landed state is *gate authored, nothing measured*.

## 1. The workload

The representative workload is R-18-004a's eight members, cited by clause and never restated, plus five transitions between them. A transition is the awkward case a minimum demonstration omits and a user meets: two members contending, or a member interrupted by an event the platform answers with a refusal, a reboot, or a loss of a service. Each is stated as a member-and-transition rather than as a scenario with a script, because the script is R-18-004d's to fix.

| Id | Member or transition | Stated against | What the limits of §2 read over it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M-i | telephony | R-18-004a(i) | the deadline class, the ringable state, the emergency compartment |
| M-ii | data | R-18-004a(ii) | the rates inside the admitted slots |
| M-iii | secure session | R-18-004a(iii) | the two handshake bounds |
| M-iv | camera | R-18-004a(iv) | the still and the burst |
| M-v | media | R-18-004a(v) | the decoded stream beside audio and the compositor, inside the declared ceiling |
| M-vi | shell | R-18-004a(vi) | every rung of R-11-021's ladder and R-11-022a's derived input-to-response bound at each |
| M-vii | inference | R-18-004a(vii) | the sustained token rate over the declared context, and §2's quality predicate |
| M-viii | content | R-18-004a(viii) | the formats opened through the handler graph |
| T-1 | a call during camera work | M-i arriving while M-iv holds its burst | M-i's deadlines and M-iv's bound both hold, and the input-to-response bound holds through the switch of focus |
| T-2 | a large incoming object while inference holds its context | M-viii's object arriving over M-ii while M-vii's context is resident | M-vii's rate holds, the object's bounded pool answers without a rung step-down, and the bound holds at the rung in force |
| T-3 | lost connectivity | M-ii's link dropping during M-i, M-iii or M-vii | the members that need no link hold, the ones that do refuse in their own units, and nothing waits on the composition service |
| T-4 | a full pool | a bounded pool reaching R-08-047's typed exhausted verdict during M-vi at its topmost rung | the refusal lands on the request that filled it, R-12-087's ladder runs, and no member's pass condition is missed by the action taken |
| T-5 | an update with durable work pending | R-13-001a's generation committing while M-viii holds unsaved work in an enumerated mutable volume | the work survives the boot R-10-026 fixes, the device returns at the rung it left, and the turnaround limit holds |

The five transitions are the ones [the critique](critique.md) names as what comfort wants beyond the floor: a call during camera work, a large incoming object while inference holds its context, lost connectivity, a full pool, and an update with durable work pending. None adds a member; a workload absent from the eight is not floor by omission and is not made floor here either.

## 2. The limits

One row per limit. *Stated against* is the clause or row the limit reads, by id; *pass* and *fail* are in the user's units and copy no figure the cited clause owns; *decided by* is the rung of R-18-004c's ladder whose instrument can reach the row, and a row is not deemed met at any earlier rung; *what would change it* names the artifact whose arrival moves the row. Where a value is this document's own, the cell names the declared parameter that carries it.

### Declared parameters

These are the numbers this document owns. Each has a ground and a stated way to be wrong, each is reprinted in the report's `manifest`, and each carries a status. **Every row's status is `proposed`, and a proposed value binds nothing**: the envelope these rows quantify is the one R-18-004a's fifth Accept says the eight members do not decide, Q10 states the arms it forces and reserves the choice to the product's owner, and this document takes no arm. A row moves to `ratified` by the product's owner's recorded decision and by nothing here; a report read against a proposed value states so in its verdict.

## 3. The record

One record per result, two renderings generated together and never authored apart: a machine-readable record the gate of §4 reads, and a Markdown document a reader reads, generated from the record so the two cannot drift. It is the freeze contract's report shape with the product columns this document adds, and it reuses that contract's `manifest` conventions rather than restating them.

### Blocks

| Block | What it carries |
| --- | --- |
| `manifest` | image hash, composer commit, Sail model revision, the devicetree and mode-schedule hash, profile revision, and the declared parameters in force with their status |
| `workload` | the §1 member id, the transition id where one applies, and the version of the demonstration set once R-18-004d fixes one, `n/a` until then |
| `quality` | the member's pass condition in the unit of the clause it feeds, and for M-vii the two quality comparators PL-9 reads and the task set they were taken over |
| `memory` | per-class resident bytes as the whole-program plan places them, each class's declared τ, and the population rung in force |
| `assumptions` | the operating point per R-11-017, and the declared per-mode power figure and the declared battery capacity, both per R-18-004c, the capacity being what R-12-065's gauging reads |
| `method` | the instrument, its revision, the predicate each count was taken with, and the uncertainty stated as a range and its ground |
| `results` | one entry per limit, carrying the four column families `host_elapsed`, `target_model_cycles`, `fpga_observed` and `silicon_measured`, each present and each `n/a` where the axis does not apply |
| `refusals` | one entry per typed exhausted verdict and per ladder action raised during the run: the pool, the request it landed on, the action taken, and whether that request is inside a member's pass condition |
| `verdict` | per limit: `pass`, `fail`, or `undecided` with the rung of R-18-004c's ladder that would decide it, and whether any value it was read against is `proposed` |

### Skeleton

```json
{
  "manifest": {
    "image_hash": "<hash of the composed image>",
    "composer_commit": "<commit>",
    "sail_model_revision": "<revision the cycle columns were taken from>",
    "devicetree_mode_schedule_hash": "<hash>",
    "profile_revision": "<revision>",
    "declared_parameters": { "DP-1": { "value": "4.0 W", "status": "proposed" } }
  },
  "workload":    { "member": "M-vi", "transition": "T-4", "demonstration_set": "n/a" },
  "quality":     { "pass_condition": "<clause unit>", "kl_divergence": "n/a", "top1_agreement": "n/a", "task_set": "n/a" },
  "memory":      { "first_class_bytes": 0, "second_class_bytes": 0, "tau": { "first": 0.0, "second": 0.0 }, "rung": 0 },
  "assumptions": { "operating_point": "<per R-11-017>", "mode_power_declared": "<per R-18-004c>", "battery_declared": "<per R-18-004c>" },
  "method":      { "instrument": "<tool>", "revision": "<commit>", "predicate": "<how each count was taken>", "uncertainty": "<range and ground>" },
  "results": [
    { "limit": "PL-1",
      "host_elapsed": "n/a", "target_model_cycles": 0, "fpga_observed": "n/a", "silicon_measured": "n/a" }
  ],
  "refusals": [
    { "pool": "<pool>", "landed_on": "<request>", "action": "<ladder action>", "inside_member": false }
  ],
  "verdict":  [ { "limit": "PL-1", "value": "undecided", "decided_by": "R-17-041 with R-11-017", "against_proposed": false } ]
}
```

### Column conventions

- **The four families are four clocks and are never summed, averaged, or converted into one another.** `host_elapsed` is wall-clock on a host, the family Q4's `llama-bench` runs and Q9's build and proof components land in; `target_model_cycles` is worst-case cycles from the timing-annotated Sail model at the revision the manifest names, no wall-clock figure until R-11-017's operating point is selected; `fpga_observed` is a clock reading on an FPGA whose clock is not the target's; `silicon_measured` is a reading on the part. A figure belongs to exactly one family, and a report placing one family's figure in another's column is rejected by §4.
- **A cell where the axis does not apply carries `n/a`**, never a blank and never a dash, on the freeze contract's ground: a blank is indistinguishable from an omitted measurement, which is the confusion the gate exists to catch.
- **Every count carries the predicate it was taken with and every figure the revision it was taken at**, the plan's own convention, carried in `method` and `manifest` rather than in prose beside the number.
- **A verdict read against a `proposed` parameter says so**, and such a verdict is a reading and not a gate result until the parameter is ratified.
- **No limit's pass value is copied into the record.** The record names the limit by id and the gate reads the limit from this document, so a limit that moves moves once.

## 4. The gate

Stated as predicates over the record of §3, so that a rejection is mechanical and its reason is nameable. As with the freeze gate, it is a gate a person runs, not a continuous-integration gate: none of the three host gates `tools/run.py` runs reads a product record, and an instrument that later runs these predicates enters through [tools/run.py](../tools/run.py) under [the tools' conventions](../tools/README.md), quarantined while the decision it serves is deferred, exactly as [the freeze instrument](../tools/quarantine/README.md) is.

| Id | The gate rejects when | Governing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PG-1 | a `manifest` field is absent, or the Sail model revision is absent where a `target_model_cycles` column carries a value | R-18-004c; the freeze contract's G-9 |
| PG-2 | a figure in one family is labelled as another, or any cell sums, averages or converts across families, a `host_elapsed` figure entering a target claim being the case Q4's runs make likely | this contract, §3 |
| PG-3 | a `verdict` of `pass` or `fail` stands on a wall-clock or deadline limit (PL-1, PL-2, PL-3, PL-7) read from a `target_model_cycles` figure while the manifest records no R-17-041 latency magnitudes or no R-11-017 artifact, a cycle count being no wall-clock figure until an operating point is selected; or read from an `fpga_observed` or `silicon_measured` figure while the `assumptions` block records no R-11-017 operating point; or read from the functional emulator at all, it reaching nothing timed | R-18-004c |
| PG-4 | a `verdict` of `pass` or `fail` stands on PL-4's measured side or on PL-5 with no `silicon_measured` figure, no pre-silicon artifact reaching joules or a junction temperature | R-18-004c |
| PG-5 | a limit is read against a figure [the estimates](performance-estimates.md) disclaim as a freeze input, or a declared parameter is filled from an estimates row, which is the bank-count contract's BD-3 refusal applied to this record | this contract, §2; the bank-count contract's BD-3 |
| PG-6 | a member's `pass` stands on a mechanism the register deletes: M-v on a codec block, M-vi on variable refresh or on slack yielded across a confidentiality boundary, M-vii on routed expert fetch across the storage boundary, M-viii on content sniffing or runtime handler registration | R-18-004a's second Accept |
| PG-7 | the `workload` block names a porting target rather than a capability, or names a member outside R-18-004a's eight as floor | R-18-004d |
| PG-8 | a `refusals` entry with `inside_member` true stands beside a `pass` on PL-6, or a ladder action in the run stepped the rung down beside a `pass` on PL-2 | R-08-047, R-12-087 |
| PG-9 | a `verdict` of `pass` on PL-8 stands on a run in which the composer was reachable, or on which any rollback or ladder action was not exercised | R-13-001c, R-16-008 |
| PG-10 | M-vii's `quality` block lacks either comparator, the two being the quantized weight's mean per-token KL divergence from and top-1 agreement with the widest weight of the same model over the declared task set, or the comparator's reference weight is not the same model | this contract, PL-9, DP-4, DP-5 |
| PG-11 | a `verdict` of `pass` or `fail` stands on a limit whose declared parameter is `proposed` without `against_proposed` set, a reading against an unratified value being a reading and not a verdict | this contract, §2 |
| PG-12 | a `memory` block lacks a class's resident bytes, its τ, or the rung, the demand side of R-18-004b's comparisons being what the record exists to make falsifiable early | R-18-004b |

Two of these carry the document's whole point. PG-2 is what keeps a host benchmark from becoming a target claim: Q4's host runs are real measurements of a host and go in `host_elapsed`, and the moment one is carried into the target's column the record says something nobody measured. PG-11 is what keeps a proposed envelope from becoming a gate by habit: a report can be read against DP-1 today, and the reading is recorded as a reading until the product's owner turns the value into a limit.

## 5. Why the gate is disjoint from admission

A candidate passing the product gate and a candidate admitted by the security architecture are different propositions, and this document is built so that neither implies the other. Every admission rule the register states reads the image, the manifest, the proof objects, the whole-program plan and the schedule: the CIC proof terms and the CHERI-TAL derivations R-13-011's tiers admit on, the admission proof R-11-017 emits, the memory plan R-08-010 places, the descriptor enumeration R-12-024f fixes. None of them reads a `results`, `refusals` or `verdict` block, because none of those exists at admission; and no predicate of §4 reads a proof object, a derivation or a plan except to require that the manifest name them. **The gate's predicates and the admission rules read disjoint columns**, which is what makes a candidate that clears every admission rule and fails a limit here a possibility rather than a contradiction.

One worked example, on paper, with no candidate measured. A composed configuration is admitted: every compartment carries its tier's certificate, the schedule at every rung of R-11-021's ladder is admission-proved with the reserved band identical across rungs, the whole-program plan fits both classes inside (1 − τ) of their capacities, the descriptor enumeration covers every format the release opens, and the eight members each meet their clause on the composed system as far as the composed system decides them. It is a correct machine by every rule this register states. Now T-1 runs on it: a call arrives during a burst. The radio's hard tasks are admitted and meet their deadlines, so M-i holds; the ISP's slot is admitted and the burst completes inside its bound, so M-iv holds; and the focus slot, sized against input cadence exactly as R-11-022a requires, meets the bound at the rung the schedule proves. What the schedule did not prove, because no admission rule states it, is the interval across the *change of focus* from the camera to the call. R-11-023 names that change: the compositor requests a focus rebinding and the kernel enacts it at a major-frame boundary by permuting the slot→tenant map, every admission property invariant under the permutation, and R-07-031b's Accept lists it among the schedule transitions enacted at that boundary; R-11-006 fits the slots to the major frame; R-17-007 adds only that the permutation timestamps the change to the unfocused. R-11-022a's bound runs from an input latched with a tenant already in focus, so an input latched before the boundary waits for the rebinding and then for the next focus visit, an interval composable from the major frame and R-11-022a's bound that no entry states as an input-to-response obligation; if the composition sized the visit period at the edge of the bound, that first response is over it. PL-2 fails. Every admission rule held, the register's own (vi) held at every rung, and the device missed a touch during a phone call, which is exactly the failure a user reports and no refusal describes. The same shape recurs at T-4, where R-12-087's ladder correctly steps the rung down to recover a pool and the topmost rung the user was at is gone, and at T-3, where a member that never needed the composer is fine and an install that does is refused, correctly, and PL-8 passes only if that was the *only* thing refused.

That is the whole of the disjointness claim, and it is demonstrable only on paper today: no image exists, M1.2's backend being open, and no wall-clock clause is decided before R-17-041's magnitudes and R-11-017's artifact exist. What this document establishes is that the gate *can* fail a candidate admission passes, not that it has.

## 6. What this document does not decide

- **No instrument.** No tool runs the predicates of §4 today, and none is written here: an instrument that runs them enters through [tools/run.py](../tools/run.py) under the tools' own conventions when a record exists to run them over, quarantined until then. A dashboard ahead of a reproducible record would be the thing the plan's Q-series refuses.
- **No measured delta and no verdict.** Every `verdict` this document can produce today is `undecided`, and every figure in the record is `n/a` until a candidate exists.
- **No register act.** The limits read register clauses by id and move none of them; the transitions of §1 are capabilities and not R-18-004d's set; and the envelope values are proposed and not ratified. A limit that needed a clause to move is reported as a register gap and not taken here.
- **No estimate moves.** A documentation correction improves accuracy and not hardware speed: the headline of [the estimates](performance-estimates.md#headline-total) is split into the claims its rows support, each naming its comparator and its evidence, and no figure there rises for this document existing.
- **The product's owner's decisions are stated as arms and none is taken.** A candidate meeting the eight members inside a fixed-power envelope is R-18-004a's other arm, a fixed-capacity secure appliance, and Q10 is where that arm is weighed; this document gives the envelope a proposed number so that the weighing has a scale, and gives it no verdict.
