at `01b86fef6a51c6d0e3a9f2e50266f7fa1db3dbce`, adds the

assumptions, followed by positive/refusal controls and the real hosted recursive
kernel join. Q35n's cold snapshot export/import and Q35b's canonical consumer
remain separate joins. The common inventory guard, allowed axiom contexts,
