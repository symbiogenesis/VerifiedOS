# The unassigned proof and specification map

*A dependency and cost map, and not a work list that commissions anything. It is Q22g's deliverable in [the implementation plan](../implementation/implementation-checklist.md#12-build-order-milestones-and-execution-state): for every obligation family the plan names as owned by no item, it states the exact endpoint, what is checked locally today, which qualified source [the proof reuse inventory](proof-reuse.md) supplies and what stands between that source and the endpoint, and then either the existing cell that owns the work or a numbered next slice with a class, a range and a predicate a lane can self-check. It is derived from [the register](../requirements-register.md), [the crown-jewel inventory](crown-jewels.md), [the field-binding map](field-bindings.md), [the coverage matrix](coverage-matrix.md) and the inventory's subject records; where it disagrees with any of them it is defective and they win. It adds no obligation, flips no inventory status, and prices no program.*

## How to read a row

- **The endpoint is a theorem statement shape and the artifact it is proved over.** A row names the Prop field of [ApexTheorem.v](../../proofs/ApexTheorem.v)'s `Vocabulary` record the obligation instantiates where one exists, the artifact the register makes its specification, and the shape the closing theorem takes over it. A **statement artifact** authors the specification as Gallina definitions with non-vacuity witnesses under R-05-165 and R-05-166 and proves nothing of the object it names; an **implementation or refinement proof** discharges a statement against an executable, a binary, a circuit or the Sail term. The two are priced apart and marked apart: `S` for a statement slice, `P` for a proof slice, `D` for a decision, `M` for a measurement.
- **A range is an engineering judgment over the remaining local obligation, on [the plan's conventions](../implementation/implementation-checklist.md#checklist-conventions).** Class `X` where the slice authors against an authority outside this repository, with its upper end at least twice its lower; class `I` where the authority is inside it, with no floor. An existing start-from receives no second discount, and a source the inventory qualifies is credited only for the transfer the slice actually makes.
- **A blocking decision is priced as its own slice and the implementation it unblocks is `unpriced` until it lands, never zero.** That is [the foundation map's](../hardware/cheri-foundation-map.md#foundation-ownership-ledger) rule and [the compiler-route contract's](../languages/compiler-route-contract.md#re-entry-packages), applied across every family here.
- **A shared prerequisite has one owner**, named once in [the first section](#1-the-shared-prerequisites-each-with-one-owner) and cited by every consumer, so no consumer's range carries it a second time.
- **The completed experiments and the source search are reused and not repeated.** Q2c's foundation ownership, Q21b's route comparison, Q22b's and Q22c's qualifications, Q22d's boundary cell and the post-M10 opening's route-coverage reading are cited where they decide; the source readings are the inventory's, dated 2026-09-10, and no upstream is rebuilt or replayed by this map.
- **What the priced slices do not reach is enumerated** in [the closing sections](#8-mandatory-work-outside-the-priced-total), with an owner where the plan names one and a register act where it does not. The sum of the slices is not a total for anything.

## 9. What this map does not decide
