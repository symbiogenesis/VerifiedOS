# Probing model interface and acceptance

This contract opens the conditional model and finite algebra authoring for the post-M10 probing-model cell. It does not qualify a new probabilistic dependency under U-20 or change R-15-053a, R-05-004a, R-17-058a or R-17-058d. The [crypto source review](proof-reuse/crypto.md) records the SSProve/FCF version and assumption barriers and the narrower scope of the Lean cardinality result. The locked package set and installed switch must be inspected before claiming any probability foundation is available.

A circuit has explicitly indexed gates, input and output shares, and register state at successive cycles. A well-formed combinational dependency points to an earlier wire. Evaluation has an explicit fuel and a proved sufficient bound. A stable probe observes its wire at one cycle. A glitch probe includes transitive combinational inputs and stops at register boundaries. A transition probe sees the pair of consecutive values at a register boundary; a difference-only observation is a distinct, weaker candidate and must have a separating example. The robust observation contains the selected glitch and transition observations. Probe order counts physical probe locations before expansion. Fixed time-indexed probe lists must not be described as adaptive strategies.

The model names PINI as the intended composition interface and separately defines NI and SNI with their input-share simulation budgets. Any implication between these definitions must carry its exact side conditions. Naming PINI and checking a gadget is not an arbitrary-circuit composition theorem. Arithmetic composition, Boolean composition, physical circuit-checker qualification and the combined fault reduction remain separately owned.

An explicit finite probability interface may expose its laws as record fields, with no new global axioms. A commutative mass monoid alone is only a weighted counting algebra. Security claims require sound observation equality and a nonempty, valid distribution. Uniform finite counting can establish equality of outcome counts on a shared nonzero denominator; it must not be represented as an imported SSProve or FCF theorem. A future probability adapter must prove normalization, nonnegativity, pushforward and reindexing laws at the consumer boundary. Empty sample spaces, zero masses and an equality test that accepts every value are rejected qualification witnesses.

Acceptance for the authorable baseline requires native compilation, constant and assumption inventories, record witnesses, and concrete success/refusal cases. It includes a general theorem that additive encoding into d shares with independent uniform masks hides the secret from any at most d-1 distinct share observations, including observations of the completing share. Two-share or three-share enumeration is supporting evidence only. It also includes an unmasked leaking circuit, a glitch observation that is strictly stronger than the stable observation, and a register-reuse transition leak. Their probability/counting premises must be inhabited. The report states which fixed or adaptive adversary interface each theorem actually covers.

R-15-053a's correspondence between this model and physical silicon remains explicit. Delay imbalance, coupling, layout effects, unmodeled leakage and attacks above the admitted order are not theorems of the Gallina circuit model. Fresh independent masks in the finite model remain a modeling premise; R-05-004a's computational DRBG connection, mask budget, execution-aware netlist evidence, physical characterization and R-17-058d's combined fault/probing reduction are separate acceptance work. The parent checklist cell stays open until its qualified probability foundation and all required semantic reviews are complete.
