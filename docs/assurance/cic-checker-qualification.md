## What is checked, and what is not

`AdmissionPath.v` provides composition-time records and decisions: `Versions`, `Step`, `Cert`, `Package`, `Machine`, `Reading`, `check_cert` and its admission properties. In particular, `Cert` contains four version codes, a binding identifier and a list of discharge records. It contains neither a serialized CIC derivation nor a parser for one. Its content-addressed identifiers are abstract natural numbers. Refining those records to bytes, digest algorithms and real derivations is still required.

The [typed assembly language](../languages/typed-assembly-language.md#7-the-frozen-theory) fixes rank-1 predicative polymorphism, syntactic type equality, no universes and no extensible inductives. Its three checker moves are bounded dataflow and structural decisions. A PCUIC conversion checker does not implement that TAL algorithm. The on-device CIC path serves the additional proof obligations; it must preserve R-05-026's final-image and committed-source binding, R-06-015a's generation budgets, and R-06-014's separate bootstrap evidence. Neither MetaRocq type checking nor this document proves a CHERI binary refinement.

## Actual source interfaces and every explicit premise

The entry constants are `MetaRocq.SafeChecker.PCUICSafeChecker.check_wf_env`, `check_wf_ext`, `check_type_wf_env`, `check_type_wf_env_bool`, `check_wf_env_bool_spec` and `typecheck_program`. They are definitions in the selected source, not invented local theorem names. `typecheck_program` returns a dependent result carrying an inferred type, an abstract environment related to the supplied environment, its well-formedness and a PCUIC inference judgment. The Boolean wrapper's success theorem concludes a squashed PCUIC typing judgment. It does not conclude a C execution, a wire decoding or a target-image property. Their [source signatures](https://github.com/MetaRocq/metarocq/blob/7d4f2fddb0e6b1ef03050999bce281237e2828f6/safechecker/theories/PCUICSafeChecker.v) make the following obligations explicit.

## Bounded foundation execution route

M6.2b-0 investigates the proved bounded partial-checker arm first. The [literature starting points](proof-reuse/languages.md#bounded-cic-starting-points) supply methods for separating conversion soundness from normalization, executable partial functions and guard specification. This is a local execution strategy, not an upstream theorem about PCUIC. The selected checker remains refused until the complete acceptance predicate is satisfied; the work remains after M8a and its estimate is unchanged.

### Corpus and source qualification

Start by exporting the required real proofs and their transitive environment closure, including opaque proof bodies, declaration types, inductive blocks, universe declarations and transparent definitions needed for conversion. Generate a feature and dependency report from those exports, with source/exporter identities, replay instructions and a freshness check against its owning inputs, rather than maintaining a handwritten count of surface `Fixpoint` declarations. Identify mutual and nested recursion, dependent elimination, primitives, universe features and computations on which retained proofs depend. Missing exports are an explicit prerequisite, not evidence of an empty supported corpus.

Use that report to freeze the supported profile and select difficult positive examples with corresponding refuting terms. Preserve the required propositions and their final-image/source commitments. A producer may simplify or translate a proof only if the accepted result still establishes the required committed proposition and all necessary correspondence is checked. Dropping opaque bodies, making computational definitions opaque, trusting precomputed normal forms or removing required recursion cannot establish containment. A profile that excludes a required term must name it and the missing feature in the replan.

For each source component actually selected, freeze an immutable revision or versioned archive, read its own license and dependency terms, record the incorporation disposition in [THIRD-PARTY.md](../../THIRD-PARTY.md) if incorporation is proposed, and build the complete compatible tuple. Keep the CPP and FSCD editions of `logrel-coq`, their PartialFun dependency and the earlier guard repository distinct from current branches and the 2026 guard specification. Audit actual entry constants and helper theorems before constructing a large dependency on them. Paper-level claims and library import graphs do not satisfy that audit.

### Profile and metatheory decision

Choose the profile against both the exported corpus and already mechanized metatheory before widening the checker. The first comparison is the CPP/FSCD `logrel-coq` calculus, whose dependent functions, pairs, natural numbers, identity types and one universe have an executable checking development; it is a candidate for reuse, not a CIC containment result. Compare it with the required PCUIC subset and an eliminator-only encoding of the required recursion. Reject a candidate that cannot preserve a required proposition or whose translation, environment construction or computation correspondence remains unproved. Existing proof coverage is a selection criterion, never permission to discard the corpus that motivated the checker.

M6.2b-0's decision record names the object theory and judgment, universe levels and cumulativity, impredicative sorts, equality and eta rules, inductive/elimination forms, recursion and guard rules, admitted primitives, and the metatheory in which each required structural or normalization theorem is checked. For each property, name the instantiated theorem and audited assumptions, or mark the missing proof explicitly. Separate acceptance soundness, bounded termination, total normalization, logical consistency and corpus usability; none is evidence for all the others. A claim of support with an unspecified universe or guard policy fails the decision.

The selected MetaRocq interface has the missing premises enumerated above; this is the actionable obstruction, without a claim that no stronger theorem can ever be published. [Conversion certification](proof-reuse/languages.md#conversion-certification-without-normalization) distinguishes sound acceptance from normalization. A proof that the bounded checker accepts only derivable judgments does not prove consistency of its own ambient logic. R-06-014 separately grounds the checker binary in bootstrap evidence and supplies neither logical consistency nor normalization. A stronger metatheory used to establish consistency must be named; it is not silently discharged by reproducible builds or DDC.

An eliminator-only object calculus can avoid a general syntactic guard in that calculus. It still owes its eliminator metatheory, corpus translation and the refinement endpoint. Retaining PCUIC fixpoints, even when a producer spells them through well-founded combinators, retains the applicable guard predicate and preservation obligations. The seven fields cease to be obligations only if a checked change of judgment and representation removes their use; this is a reviewed alternative to the selected route, not a completed proof of its fields.

Set-theoretic models are not ruled out wholesale by Reynolds' obstruction to a particular interpretation of polymorphism. Lee and Werner's [proof-irrelevant model of CC with predicative induction and judgmental equality](https://lmcs.episciences.org/920) is a concrete counterexample to that blanket claim: it uses ZF with countably many inaccessible cardinals. Its calculus and assumptions are not automatically those of this profile, so it supplies neither an undeclared local axiom nor a qualification of full Rocq.

**Universe levels and cumulativity.** The profile admits the predicative `Type` hierarchy with algebraic levels and the constraint graph, the cumulativity `prop_sub_type=true` carries, and template universe polymorphism. Template polymorphism is a measured requirement rather than a convenience: 46 symbols carry it, `AdmissionPath.Build_Generation` among them, so a profile admitting only monomorphic universes refuses a constructor this corpus already compiles, while one admitting only quantified polymorphism implements a rule no symbol here uses. Cumulative inductive types are outside the profile: no symbol is polymorphic in the quantified sense and no source declares `Cumulative` or `Polymorphic`. **Impredicative sorts.** `Prop` is impredicative and `Set` is predicative, which is the pinned prover's default and the configuration this corpus compiles under. The apex statement needs the first and not incidentally: `ApexTheorem.T` quantifies `forall C : v.(Compartment) -> Prop` over a record `v : Vocabulary` whose `graph_permits` field has type `(Compartment -> Prop) -> Prop`, so the proposition is a second-order quantification inside `Prop` over a field's type. `prop_sub_type` is a separate rule from impredicativity and is retained at its frozen value, a change to it being a change of judgment.

**Equality and eta rules.** Judgmental equality is PCUIC conversion with cumulativity: beta, delta over transparent constants, iota, zeta, and eta for functions. Definitional proof irrelevance is outside the profile, no source declaring `SProp`. Propositional equality is the ordinary inductive identity type: no source spells `Equations` or a UIP setting, and the corpus's own uses of equality reach `eq_ind`, `eq_ind_r` and `eq_sym` in the closure rather than an axiom.

**Inductive and elimination forms.** The profile admits inductive families with parameters and indices, record types with projections, and dependent elimination with an annotated return predicate. **Recursion and guard rules.** The guard has to cover single, possibly nested, structural fixpoints over those families. **Whether eliminators remove the guard interface or encode recursion inside it.** Both sentences are true of different things and the decision needs them apart. An eliminator-only calculus removes the interface: with no `fix` node, `GuardChecker` and the seven `GuardCheckerCorrect` fields have nothing to range over and stop being premises of its typing judgment, because the recursion is encoded inside the eliminator's own iota rule and its termination argument is discharged once per inductive block by positivity rather than once per definition by a syntactic test. It does not remove the obligation: the corpus is fixed, so each recursive definition has to be re-expressed as an eliminator application and each re-expression proved to establish the same proposition. The seven fields are therefore replaced by an eliminator metatheory and a translation correspondence, not deleted. Retaining PCUIC fixpoints keeps the fields, and spelling a fixpoint through a well-founded combinator is not a re-expression: it is still a `fix` node to the kernel.

## Bounded wire and arena contract

The proposed checker input is one immutable envelope containing: format and checker-profile versions; the artifact, source-closure, Sail, specification and theorem-environment commitments; the generation's closed step and working-set numerals; the closed expected proposition; and one serialized term with its ordered global declarations and universe data. Every commitment is checked before a verdict is bound to the candidate image. A claim supplied by the candidate is not itself evidence that the expected proposition is the required tier theorem.

The candidate wire language is a canonical, length-delimited encoding of the selected PCUIC constructors: bound indices, sorts and universe instances, products, lambdas, let bindings, application, references to declared constants and inductives, constructors, cases, projections and structurally guarded fixpoints. Names use a declared byte encoding; constructor tags, declaration order, binder annotations, relevance, universe constraints and all argument-list lengths have one accepted representation. Integers use minimal unsigned encodings except explicitly signed universe offsets. References designate already available declarations or the enclosing mutual block, never host pointers. Unknown tags, unresolved variables/evars, undeclared primitives, opaque proofs absent from the committed environment, noncanonical encodings, duplicate names, trailing bytes and invalid indices refuse. Coinduction, primitive floats, arrays and strings are outside this proposed first subset. This is a contract for a future reader, not a claim that current emitted proofs all fit it; closing that containment obligation requires the real exported proof corpus.

The generation declares maximum envelope bytes, term nodes, nesting, global declarations, names, universe levels/constraints, indices and numerals, plus total checking steps and arena bytes. Check lengths and arithmetic before allocation or traversal, including multiplication and alignment overflow. Parsing, environment construction, type checking and conversion all consume the declared budgets. Exhaustion returns refusal with the artifact unadmitted. Reducing either budget cannot enlarge acceptance. A bound is a delivery parameter, not a replacement for a typing or normalization premise.

Use one bounded bump arena for one complete term check, with an explicit bounded work stack. No general allocator, collector, retained pointer or partial derivation escapes. On success, failure, malformed input or exhaustion, copy only the fixed verdict and its input commitments into the output record, then wipe the entire arena before reuse. The capability boundary is read-only candidate/certificate/profile and write-only verdict, with no access to content-store or transactor authority. Initialization, allocation, wipe and failure paths require concrete memory and zeroization proofs. This contract neither changes TAL's separate canonical one-pass reader nor borrows its linear complexity bound for CIC conversion.

Positive fixtures must serialize a closed identity/application, a dependent product and its application, a valid inductive elimination, and a guarded recursive proof from the required real corpus. Negative fixtures must cover an ill-typed application, invalid binder/reference, inconsistent universes, an unguarded fixpoint, unknown/duplicate/trailing encoding, wrong commitment, and each budget boundary at, below and above the required cost. No such fixtures are reported passed: this source exposes typed AST interfaces and no qualified local wire reader. Fabricating a small unrelated parser would not exercise this predicate.
