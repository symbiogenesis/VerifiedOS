## 1. Decision and spending boundary

Neither supported usable payload nor sustained fixed-grant bandwidth for that stack can be established from them. This is the deciding absence; it is not evidence that the device family is impossible, or that a supplier has refused a request. No supplier was contacted, agreement signed, PDK acquired, specimen ordered or characterization commissioned.

Q6a's early decision is complete at this boundary. Its **credible** arm is withheld. Return the decision to **Q10 before broad laptop-specific Q34f/g/h or Q41a/b implementation**. Their contract and independent-interface preparation, and scalar M8a/M8b work, can continue under their existing predicates. Q6b, R5, R5a and M9a remain open. This record selects no replacement product and changes no release requirement.
