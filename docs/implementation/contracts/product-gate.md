# The Product-Gate Contract: Absolute Limits, the Result Record, and What Rejects a Candidate

> Non-normative, governed by [R-18-004a](../../requirements-register.md), and a companion to [the performance estimates](../../performance/performance-estimates.md).
> It decides nothing today. It states the absolute pass and fail limits a candidate is measured against on one representative workload, the record every result is written into, and the predicates that reject a report or a candidate, all authored before any candidate exists so that no limit here can have been chosen to make a particular candidate pass.
> Where this document and [the register](../../requirements-register.md) disagree, the register wins and this document is defective. Where it and [the estimates](../../performance/performance-estimates.md) disagree, the estimates are a non-normative estimate and this document is a gate over measurements, so neither corrects the other: a limit here is stated against a row there by its id and copies no figure from it.

## How to read this

R-18-004a requires eight workload members to run together on one composed configuration, each at a specified quality. R-18-004c names the gate and evidence needed to evaluate each clause. Meeting that floor and every security admission rule does not by itself establish a usable product: a refusal can protect security while interrupting the user's work. Authored under Q1 of [the implementation plan](../implementation-checklist.md), this contract defines that product failure before candidates are measured. It sets **absolute** limits in user-facing units for response latency, sustained power and temperature, capacity refusals, update turnaround, and operation without the external composition service. Its workload includes transitions beyond the floor's minimum demonstrations.

Three rules govern the limits:

- **Cite existing figures by their owning clause or row.** Limits reference register clauses and estimates rows by ID, without repeating their numbers. [The checker's compounds group](../../../tools/check-rules.md) checks the estimates' arithmetic. Numbers this contract owns appear in one declared-parameters table with their status.
- **Use the evidence required for the quantity being judged.** A cycle count becomes a wall-clock bound only with R-17-041's latency magnitudes and R-11-017's selected operating point and slot share. Each §2 row names the stage of R-18-004c's evidence ladder that can decide it. Section 4 rejects a report that claims a verdict from an earlier, insufficient instrument.
- **Proposed envelope values are not binding limits.** Sustained power, temperature and update turnaround belong to the physical envelope that R-18-004a's fifth acceptance criterion leaves outside the pre-silicon gate. Q10 reserves the resulting product choices to the product's owner. The declared parameters in §2 bind a candidate only after that owner ratifies them.

R-18-004d's versioned demonstration set supplies the per-member details: live compartments, population rung, the desktop domain's live members with their weights and its reference interactive member, composited surfaces, radio state, input cadence and pass condition. It must be fixed before R-15-108's exploration. Section 1 defines capabilities and transitions without fixing those details.
This contract specifies the result format and rejection rules, following [the freeze contract's report and gate](freeze-measurement.md#7-the-report) and [the bank-count contract's inadmissibility table](../../hardware/bank-count-dse-contract.md#5-what-makes-a-report-inadmissible). It implements no measurement instrument and contains no candidate measurements. Q1's completion means *gate authored, nothing measured*.

## 1. The workload

The representative workload is R-18-004a's eight members, cited by clause and never restated, plus seven transitions between them. A transition is the awkward case a minimum demonstration omits and a user meets: two members contending, or a member interrupted by an event the platform answers with a refusal, a reboot, or a loss of a service. Each is stated as a member-and-transition rather than as a scenario with a script, because the script is R-18-004d's to fix.

None adds a member to the eight-member workload floor.

These are the numbers this document owns. A row moves to `ratified` by the product's owner's recorded decision and by nothing here; a report read against a proposed value states so in its verdict.

## 3. The record

One record per result, two renderings generated together and never authored apart: a machine-readable record the gate of §4 reads, and a Markdown document a reader reads, generated from the record so the two cannot drift. It is the freeze contract's report shape with the product columns this document adds, and it reuses that contract's `manifest` conventions rather than restating them.

### Blocks

| Block | What it carries |
| --- | --- |
| `manifest` | image hash, composer commit, Sail model revision, the devicetree and mode-schedule hash, profile revision, and the declared parameters in force with their status |
| `workload` | the §1 member id, the transition id where one applies, and the version of the demonstration set once R-18-004d fixes one, `n/a` until then |
| `quality` | the member's pass condition in the unit of the clause it feeds, and for M-vii the two quality comparators PL-9 reads and the task set they were taken over |
| `memory` | per-class resident bytes as the whole-program plan places them, each class's declared τ, the population rung in force, and the desktop pool's per-class extent and occupancy |
| `assumptions` | the operating point per R-11-017, and the declared per-mode power figure and the declared battery capacity, both per R-18-004c, the capacity being what R-12-065's gauging reads |
| `method` | the instrument, its revision, the predicate each count was taken with, and the uncertainty stated as a range and its ground |
| `results` | one entry per limit, carrying the four column families `host_elapsed`, `target_model_cycles`, `fpga_observed` and `silicon_measured`, each present and each `n/a` where the axis does not apply |
| `app_iteration` | for T-7: source edit and closure, bundle/engine/profile/ABI identities, host count and live load, old/new epochs and commit result; cold/reused phase timings and consent wait; cache identity/invalidation, native-service comparator and copy costs; shared/private/staging/quarantine peaks; `n/a` for other transitions |

### Column conventions

- **The four families are four clocks and are never summed, averaged, or converted into one another.** `host_elapsed` is wall-clock on a host, the family Q4's `llama-bench` runs and Q9's build and proof components land in; `target_model_cycles` is worst-case cycles from the timing-annotated Sail model at the revision the manifest names, no wall-clock figure until R-11-017's operating point is selected; `fpga_observed` is a clock reading on an FPGA whose clock is not the target's; `silicon_measured` is a reading on the part. A figure belongs to exactly one family, and a report placing one family's figure in another's column is rejected by §4.
- **A cell where the axis does not apply carries `n/a`**, never a blank and never a dash, on the freeze contract's ground: a blank is indistinguishable from an omitted measurement, which is the confusion the gate exists to catch.
- **Every count carries the predicate it was taken with and every figure the revision it was taken at**, the plan's own convention, carried in `method` and `manifest` rather than in prose beside the number.
- **A verdict read against a `proposed` parameter says so**, and such a verdict is a reading and not a gate result until the parameter is ratified.
- **No limit's pass value is copied into the record.** The record names the limit by id and the gate reads the limit from this document, so a limit that moves moves once.

## 4. The gate

Stated as predicates over the record of §3, so that a rejection is mechanical and its reason is nameable. As with the freeze gate, it is a gate a person runs, not a continuous-integration gate: none of the three host gates `tools/run.py` runs reads a product record, and an instrument that later runs these predicates enters through [tools/run.py](../../../tools/run.py) under [the tools' conventions](../../../tools/README.md), quarantined while the decision it serves is deferred, exactly as [the freeze instrument](../../../tools/quarantine/README.md) is.

| PG-12 | a `memory` block lacks a class's resident bytes, its τ, the rung, or the desktop pool's extent and occupancy, the demand side of R-18-004b's comparisons being what the record exists to make falsifiable early | R-18-004b, R-08-047a |
| PG-13 | PL-11 claims a complete result without the `app_iteration` identities, cold/reused phase timings, explicit consent wait, concurrent load or transition-memory peaks; or its lifecycle verdict is used to claim its timing side without target evidence | R-14-013h; this contract, PL-11 |

Two of these carry the document's whole point. PG-2 is what keeps a host benchmark from becoming a target claim: Q4's host runs are real measurements of a host and go in `host_elapsed`, and the moment one is carried into the target's column the record says something nobody measured. PG-11 is what keeps a proposed envelope from becoming a gate by habit: a report can be read against DP-1 today, and the reading is recorded as a reading until the product's owner turns the value into a limit.

## 5. Why the gate is disjoint from admission

A candidate passing the product gate and a candidate admitted by the security architecture are different propositions, and this document is built so that neither implies the other. Every admission rule the register states reads the image, the manifest, the proof objects, the whole-program plan and the schedule: the CIC proof terms and the CHERI-TAL derivations R-13-011's tiers admit on, the admission proof R-11-017 emits, the memory plan R-08-010 places, the descriptor enumeration R-12-024f fixes. None of them reads a `results`, `refusals` or `verdict` block, because none of those exists at admission; and no predicate of §4 reads a proof object, a derivation or a plan except to require that the manifest name them. **The gate's predicates and the admission rules read disjoint columns**, which is what makes a candidate that clears every admission rule and fails a limit here a possibility rather than a contradiction.

One worked example, on paper, with no candidate measured. A composed configuration is admitted: every compartment carries its tier's certificate, the fixed tier's schedule is admission-proved and the desktop domain's envelope admits as one tenant under R-11-006c with its derived focus dispatch bound inside R-18-004a(vi)'s, the whole-program plan fits both classes inside (1 − τ) of their capacities with the desktop pool charged whole, the descriptor enumeration covers every format the release opens, and the eight members each meet their clause on the composed system as far as the composed system decides them. It is a correct machine by every rule this register states. Now T-1 runs on it: the user starts a video call while a build runs in the desktop. The audio path's hard tasks are fixed-tier and meet their deadlines, so M-i holds; the ISP's slot is admitted and every webcam frame lands, so M-iv holds; and R-11-006c's bound holds for whichever member is focused. What admission did not prove, because no entry states it, is the interval across the *change of focus* from the editor to the call. R-07-037i names that change: the compositor's focus request reaches the domain's session manager, which forwards it to the kernel, which enacts it at the affected member's next dispatch point. R-11-006c's bound runs from an input latched with a member already in focus, so an input latched before the focus change is enacted waits for the request's own path and then for the new focused member's dispatch, an interval no entry states as an input-to-response obligation; and the build's members, at the declared weights, can hold the domain up to their EEVDF lag before the new focus weight takes effect. If the composition declared requests at the edge of the bound, that first response is over it. PL-2 fails. Every admission rule held, the register's own (vi) held at every admissible load, and the laptop missed a click as a call started, which is exactly the failure a user reports and no refusal describes. The same shape recurs at T-4, where R-08-047d's ladder correctly hibernates the least-recently-focused permitted member to recover the pool and the document the user was about to return to is gone from memory, and at T-3, where a member that never needed the composer is fine and an install that does is refused, correctly, and PL-8 passes only if that was the *only* thing refused.

That is the whole of the disjointness claim, and it is demonstrable only on paper today: no image exists, M1.2's backend being open, and no wall-clock clause is decided before R-17-041's magnitudes and R-11-017's artifact exist. What this document establishes is that the gate *can* fail a candidate admission passes, not that it has.

## 6. What this document does not decide

- **No instrument.** No tool runs the predicates of §4 today, and none is written here: an instrument that runs them enters through [tools/run.py](../../../tools/run.py) under the tools' own conventions when a record exists to run them over, quarantined until then. A dashboard ahead of a reproducible record would be the thing the plan's Q-series refuses.
- **No measured delta and no verdict.** Every `verdict` this document can produce today is `undecided`, and every figure in the record is `n/a` until a candidate exists.
- **No register act.** The limits read register clauses by id and move none of them; the transitions of §1 are capabilities and not R-18-004d's set; and the envelope values are proposed and not ratified. A limit that needed a clause to move is reported as a register gap and not taken here.
- **No estimate moves.** A documentation correction improves accuracy and not hardware speed: the headline of [the estimates](../../performance/performance-estimates.md#headline-total) is split into the claims its rows support, each naming its comparator and its evidence, and no figure there rises for this document existing.
- **The product's owner's decisions are stated as arms and none is taken.** A candidate meeting the eight members inside a fixed-power envelope is R-18-004a's other arm, a fixed-capacity secure appliance, and Q10 is where that arm is weighed; this document gives the envelope a proposed number so that the weighing has a scale, and gives it no verdict.
