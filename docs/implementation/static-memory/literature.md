# Static-memory literature adoption

This is the disposition of the static-memory material in *Reusable Research for
VerifiedOS and Vela*, the supplied review with literature cutoff 13 September
2026. Entry numbers below identify that review's catalog, not new requirements.
The scope includes allocation, optimization certificates, bounded resource
contracts, reclamation, and the timing or representation premises that determine
when physical storage can be reused. General language design, proof automation,
cryptography and compiler construction are outside this work.

The implementation dispositions belong to Q5b's [static-memory research comparison](../../background/static-memory-research.md)
and Q22's reuse qualification. Source qualification and terms remain in the
[foundations](../../assurance/proof-reuse/foundations.md) and
[systems](../../assurance/proof-reuse/systems.md) records. Q27 owns the certified-search
policy disposition and the assignment of newly qualified sources to consumers.
This table neither completes Q27 nor changes the requirements register.

The portable LRAT experiment is evidence-producing machinery under R-05-011b:
its external verdict grounds no admitted placement, infeasibility or refinement
claim. R-05-015 and R-05-016 require a kernel-rechecked term for such acceptance;
this experiment produces no Rocq term for its serialized LRAT instance. Q27a's
refusal of an imported admission checker therefore remains in force, alongside
R-05-020's anchor conditions and R-05-065/R-05-066's existing-checker condition.
R-05-104 and R-05-105 also continue to govern production optimizer admission.

**Integrated artifact** means an actual upstream implementation is invoked in the
portable research tools, with no production admission implied. **Adapted idea** means this repository implements the
specified technique and owns its new proof obligations; it does not mean an
upstream proof has been imported. **Existing experiment** identifies executable
or mechanized work already in this tree. **Alternative** is a concrete substitute
for the portable experiment, not an admitted dependency or a candidate waiting to
be installed. The foundation record and Q27a decide its production disposition.
**Deferred** names
the missing semantic or deployment premise and the work that would establish it.
No entry inherits a paper's theorem merely because the table cites it.

## What acceptance means

The [candidate adapter](candidates.md), [certificate path](certificates.md)
and [resource contract](resource-contracts.md) each own their replay
commands, supported inputs, source identities, negative cases and remaining proof
boundary. These artifacts carry the measurements; this table does not copy their
changing result counts or spans. The [portable planner guide](../portable-memory-planner.md)
remains the common model and ordinary placement interface.

The contract-to-instance relation, layout feasibility, objective exclusion and
target reuse are separate obligations. An external UNSAT certificate cannot prove
that the frontend supplied every real program path. A modeled bounded release
trace cannot prove a device's worst-case completion time. The absence of those
joins is a concrete reason to retain the source and target qualification work,
not a reason to reimplement a generic solver or claim that a tested example
closes a production requirement.

Q5b owns comparison against the actual composed roster and compiled service
contracts. Q22 owns physical holder coverage, completion, revocation and safe
reuse. Broader Vela ownership/effect/metatheory and compiler papers remain in the
[language verification strategy](../../languages/verification-strategy.md); this
static-memory disposition does not pronounce those independent integrations done.
