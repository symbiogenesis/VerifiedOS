# Critique

*Non-normative. The canonical critique, in three movements: a gap catalogue for the current state of [spec.md](spec.md) and its companions; a review of the evaluated alternatives, their dispositions, and the lineage in [architectural-alternatives.md](architectural-alternatives.md) and [inspirations.md](inspirations.md); and a review of the register machinery itself. All of it is checked against the artifacts: the atomic-requirements register and the seven derived views. Where an entry here and the register disagree, the register wins and this document is defective.*

*References are symbolic: requirement IDs, crown-jewel rows, and section headings, never line numbers, per the register's **traces are bookmarks, not line numbers** rule; a line number is a derived fact restated in a second artifact with nothing checking it, and it goes stale on the next edit to the artifact it cites. Counted figures a document owns are either recomputed by `tools/check.py` from that artifact or stated loosely on purpose; a hand-restated exact figure is a derived fact with nothing checking it. When a finding closes or a work-list item lands, delete it, renumber where the numbering is positional, and retarget every cross-reference that pointed at it: no departure notes, no strikethroughs, no closed section; git history is the narrative, and what stands here is the open agenda. The same rule governs a simplification that is adopted or declined outright, and a partly adopted one keeps only the part still in question.*

The review machinery makes the project's real position **countable**, and it is worse than the prose reads. Twenty-six crown-jewel specifications are named; **two** are authored. Ten theorem targets are named; **none** can start, because each is premised on a specification that does not exist. Every hedge the design deleted was deleted on the strength of a verified primary, and **no primary is verified**.

**Not one of the numbered findings below is a legibility problem.** A legibility gap closes by writing a paragraph, and no finding does. What does close by writing, or by a call somebody has to make, is not charged as a finding; it stands on the work list, priced by what closing it costs.

## The dominant fact, which is not a finding

**Every hedge is spent, no primary is verified, and the interval between is claimed at the tier it holds.** This is stated where it belongs, so it is not a defect and not an entry. It sits ahead of the findings because it is the single most important true thing about the project's assurance position, and because several of the findings below are read against it.

The *verify rather than hedge* rule is applied consistently and its logic is sound at the fixed point: a mechanism whose only job is to catch the failure of a formally verified primary buys little against the cost of a second modeled mechanism. On that rule the design deletes the MMU, PMP, the S/U rings, the IOMMU, the initialization-tag plane, MTE, shadow stacks, the Harvard split, and every disjoint backstop on the machine (R-17-037, R-17-045). §17 states the hedge that remains: "the **formal verification of CHERI itself**," resting on "the **RTL ⊑ Sail** arrow … and its Coq-native restatement for the CHERI-RISC-V profile as the residual." That arrow is the layer the same document calls **the least-built layer of the stack** (R-17-039, R-18-010). The CHERI-TAL soundness metatheorem that replaces the initialization-tag plane is theorem target `CJ-TAL-SOUND`, premised on the ISA profile and **not started**. The certifying compiler, the on-device checker, the Cerise universal contract, the secure-compilation theorem: none exists.

So the rule is sound at the fixed point and its premise is false everywhere before it. On the entire trajectory to that fixed point (a multi-year interval on this roster, and the only state the project has ever occupied) the primaries are *unverified*, a hedge against an unverified primary is ordinary defense in depth, and any device that executes in that interval runs with the elegance and without the argument. A single CHERI logic error in monotonicity, revocation, or the tag plane fails every isolation boundary on the machine at once, with nothing beneath it and, until `CJ-TAL-SOUND` and the Coq refinement close, no proof above it.

**The interim claim is the knob that turns, and it is the only one that can.** Demanding retained backstops asks for the wrong thing: MMU, PMP, the S/U rings, the IOMMU, MTE, and the Harvard split are **silicon**, so "retain until the theorem closes" means fabricating them, modelling them in Sail, and paying the second-modeled-mechanism cost the rule exists to price, and R-18-002 forecloses a capability-degraded interim by name. What can move is what the interval claims for itself, and §1 carries that on G2 (R-01-002a, R-01-002b): the software premise admits no interval, since nothing runs uncertified and a missing certificate costs delivery rather than admission; the hardware premise stands at **evidence tier** (rvfi, Sail-generated SystemVerilog under commercial FEV, the imported-core netlist audit) rather than **theorem tier** (the Kami/Kôika Coq refinement); every FPGA and early-silicon unit asserts G2 at that tier and no higher; the tier rises per core when that core's unbounded refinement is admitted, never at an intermediate rung; and an attestation or datasheet asserting G2 without its tier is a review-gate finding. §3 carries the inheritance (R-03-006). The retirement condition is R-18-010's rungs, on R-05-022's two-list discipline.

**What the tier statement buys is legibility, not safety, and the exposure is untouched.** The interval is multi-year and the tier is the weaker one for all of it; what a reader of §1 and §3 alone learns is that this is so. Two consequences run into the findings below. Whether a future datasheet honours R-01-002b is a semantic question `check.py` cannot reach, which is finding 2. And the tier claim is load-bearing for exactly as long as the interval lasts, which makes it the easiest thing in this catalogue to let go stale.

## What is not built

**1. Nothing load-bearing is built, and the specification is precise enough to say how little.** [crown-jewels.md](crown-jewels.md) is the instrument that says so, and its status column reads: of twenty-six crown-jewel specifications, **two are authored** (the frozen ISA profile and the apex theorem's machine-checked statement), three are partial, **twenty-one are not authored**. The ten theorem targets each depend on a row above; twenty-one of those premises do not exist, so most of the proof program cannot begin rather than merely being unfinished.

The repository's own ledger sharpens this, and what it sharpens is *which kind* of artifact exists rather than how much of it there is. Some seven weeks, seven-hundred-odd commits, and three-and-a-half megabytes of design documents stand against a tree whose non-Markdown paths are, by size: the curated Sail model, vendored from its upstream pin with a project-authored layer over it (the dialect's custom encodings, the platform instructions, the frozen Keccak unit, the core-class and memory-class tables, the timing annotations); the consistency checker, and the assembler that stands in for a toolchain nobody has re-homed yet; a differential corpus of two dozen hand-written purecap programs that assemble to ELF and run to a HTIF verdict on the emulator generated from that model; the apex theorem's machine-checked statement with its assumption gate, and the seam witnesses beside it; and four pinned upstream submodules, which are start-froms rather than authored code. **Every one of those is a specification, an instrument, or evidence about one, and not one is the system.** No kernel, no firmware, no init, no filesystem, no crypto core, no userland, and no compiler backend: what runs on this machine today is a test harness proving that the machine's *description* is self-consistent. The loose figures are deliberate: every exact count in this catalogue sits under a `check.py` claim, and no claim holds these.

The project's only demonstrated velocity is in producing obligations. That is a defensible way to spend an early phase, and §18 and R-01-003 are candid that the as-existing assurance is low. What no artifact states is the ratio's direction: the obligation count grows monotonically and the discharge count is two. Every hardening that makes the design safer adds a crown jewel, and nothing in the process retires one except authoring it; the inventory declares its status column a **burn-down with exactly that direction stated**, the implementation plan's milestones as its retirement mechanism, so the open question is no longer the instrument but whether the discharge count moves.

## The review gate audits its own reflection

**2. The gate is referential and the risk is semantic.** §5's release gate exists because "a proof against a wrong spec verifies perfectly," and R-18-034 states plainly that the gate "audits the register rather than this prose," so an unverified extraction stands between the reviewer and the design of record and is declared the authoritative object. That an automated checker cannot decide whether a statement is the *right* statement is a property of checkers, not a repairable defect, and charging it as a gap would be a category error. What is chargeable is the residue, in three pieces.

**The instrument is strong and the wrong shape.** `check.py` checks **referential integrity**: cited bookmarks resolve, requirements carry traces, no view drops a requirement in its bearing subsections, no asserted count disagrees with its artifact, no document carries encoding damage. Its reach includes this document: every count asserted here is anchored in the checker's registry and recomputed, so an edition of this critique cannot quietly disagree with the artifacts about a number. A requirement can nonetheless resolve perfectly, trace perfectly, appear in every view, count correctly, and state the wrong thing.

**Face one: part of the review budget is spent on the document rather than the machine, and the class wants a tag.** A class of acceptance criteria is discharged by inspecting the prose rather than the artifact: *"the axiom is stated in §6 and §17, not implied"* (R-06-014); *"the decision is stated as a design choice with its consequence, not omitted"* (R-10-011); *"the consent TCB is named in §6 rather than hidden in userland"* (R-06-018); *"the split is stated rather than blurred"* (R-13-023); *"the cost is booked rather than omitted"* (R-12-020); *"the trade is stated in §15, §12, and here"* (R-17-051). How large the class is depends on where the line is drawn, so the honest figure is a band with its rule attached: counting every criterion whose test uses *stated*, *booked*, *named*, *recorded*, or *declared* while citing neither `check.py` nor a companion artifact yields **157 of 1346 acceptance criteria**; requiring the criterion to cite a `§` as the thing a reviewer opens narrows it to **31**. Both are snapshots and lower bounds, since amendments keep adding entries of exactly this class. These entries are **not** defects under R-05-153: that rule catches a claim that cannot be restated as an atomic, testable requirement, and "the consent TCB is named in §6" is atomic and testable by inspection, which is a test. The defect is one layer over, in R-05-152 making the register the audited object: a criterion that is a fact about the commentary spends the gate's scarcest resource, a reviewer's attention on the one pass that decides whether the specification says the right thing, on editorial hygiene. The repair is a **tag rather than a defect entry**: mark the spec-hygiene entries as such, so the review can price them instead of discovering them one at a time.

**The amendment rate is the same finding from the other side.** The git log is the measure: repairs routinely land within a day of the finding that names them. That velocity is the strongest evidence in the repository that the specification workstream is real, and it is the problem: a corpus amended faster than anyone re-reads it is a corpus whose review is always of a previous edition.

**Face two: the extraction-defect list opens on the wrong axis, and its own stated class is empty.** The list is empty, and the register is right that "an empty list is not the same as a swept one." But look at what the list is instrumented to catch. Its findings arrive from building a derived view and machine-checking it, which reports an enumeration two documents disagree about, never a passage that would not decompose, because nothing re-reads the prose against that question. **R-05-153's actual class, a normative claim that would not decompose, has zero entries**: not one of the 1346 requirements has yet been booked as standing for a passage that resisted restatement, and face one's entries are not the missing ones, because they decomposed fine, into requirements about the document. So two readings stand. Either the prose was atomic throughout, implausible for a document whose §17 entries routinely run five clauses deep with three parenthetical qualifications, or the extraction **accommodated** the prose where it should have confronted it. The register does not claim otherwise: its standing instruction is that sweep 2's question "has not been asked exhaustively, so further instances should be assumed present rather than absent." The channel that reports what the *prose* refused to decompose is not running, and an agenda drawn from enumerations alone means the meeting R-18-035 calls the review gate's agenda has barely started.

**Face three: the derived views can be wrong in the half nothing checks, and there are more of them every month.** [crown-jewels.md](crown-jewels.md) is declared "Normative as a **view**." The *quantitative* half of that class is closed the only way such things close: `check.py` parses the status column into its three classes, recomputes the ratio, and fails where a document asserts a figure the artifact does not bear. The *qualitative* half, which is most of it, is untouched: whether a row's `partial` is doing work `not authored` should do, whether a cited requirement is the *right* ground, whether a discharge mode in [coverage-matrix.md](coverage-matrix.md) reports what its cited requirements actually carry, all invisible by construction. Every artifact that arrives widens that surface, machine-checked for **presence** and none for **truth**, and a view is normative whether or not it has been read. The only lever this finding has is substitution: *whether R-17-030a's seams are the right ones* is not answerable, but it is **replaced** by a mechanical question it implies, whether every requirement using the vocabulary of refusal is conferred or dispositioned (finding 3). That substitution is available wherever a qualitative claim has a lexical shadow it can be held against, and most of the list above has none.

**3. A membership claim is decidable against its own members and undecidable against the world.** R-17-016 closes the crown-jewel list by conferral: membership is asserted by each requirement that has it, [crown-jewels.md](crown-jewels.md) is the one place the conferrals are collected, and `check.py` fails on a conferral with no row or a row with no requirement. R-17-030r and R-10-013a carry the identical rule: a requirement specifying a mechanism whose failure action is to stop records the refusal against itself on a `· Fail-closed:` property line, a requirement placing state under the monotonic counter records it on a `· RoT-fresh:` line, the R-17-030 seams and R-10-013 collect them, and the checker fails on a conferral no collection carries and on a seam standing under no conferral. Thirty conferrals against seventeen seams, four against one enumeration, both directions decidable. What no checker can grep for is not membership; it is the *predicate*, and conferral does not need to.

**Conferral closes completeness for none of the three sets, and the registers say so.** A specification that ought to confer crown-jewel status and does not is exactly as invisible as a refusal that ought to confer and does not; what conferral closes is the *collection's disagreement with the requirements*, which is the smaller property. Each register claims the decidable half in its own text and declines the other, which is the only honest form of the claim.

**The machinery is not the result, and a completeness convention fails exactly where it is most expensive.** Against the completeness residue the checker carries the only instrument available to it: the vocabulary of refusal is over-approximated across every requirement body, and each candidate it catches must confer, be collected, or be dispositioned by name with a reason. The scan earns its keep: the seam register carries four members the enumeration alone missed, and one of them is the sharpest member in it. **Every fault detector on this machine answers detection by stopping** (uncorrectable ECC, tag integrity, the control-flow signature on the three protected sequences, the sentinel's lockstep comparator, residual synchronizer failure), each of them right to stop, and the adversary who provokes them is the radiated-EMI and electromagnetic-fault-injection adversary §3 admits by name and §15 shields against. A probe held near the enclosure stops a device that is behaving correctly, needs no software access at all, and gives R-17-030l's life-safety composition a fourth cause: a user who cannot place an emergency call because a detector caught an injected fault. The other three members are the entropy health test, whose sole failure action is fail-stop and whose meeting is every operation needing fresh randomness; the display path, whose visible blank is a caught fail-stop on the surface the consent prompt is drawn on, reaching denial of the prompt from a direction the compositor seam does not cover; and budget admission refusal, which also costs R-17-030e its claim to be the only member whose denial cannot reach a running unit.

**What stands is the residue.** The vocabulary agenda is lexical, proves no totality, and is defeated by an author who rewords rather than decides; the dispositions are judgments recorded in a tool; neither is a quantifier over the semantic predicate, because there is none to build. So the only thing worth building is the agenda that forces a person to decide, on every run, for every candidate the vocabulary can reach. Thirty-five candidates stand dispositioned against the current registers. The thirteen on the RoT-fresh side all resolved the same way, that they enforce the floor or provide the counter and place no state under it, which is the only evidence in this repository that an enumeration closed by convention was right.

## Deletion has no opposing artifact

**4. The first release has no stated minimum viable capability, so deletion is unfalsifiable.** Assemble what §18 commits to for the first parts: **no browser**, deferred as the largest porting program; two static memory classes rather than one, the first at **1–2 GB** of planar single-tier SRAM where R-15-163's grading does not come back and the second the oxide-deck bulk that carries framebuffers, origin arenas, media buffers and model weights, whose own capacity waits on a qualification measurement nobody has taken (R-15-247, R-15-247s, R-15-173a, R-15-247m); six C-class cores with software rendering at initial bring-up; no JIT anywhere; no fixed-function GPU; a few-active-things machine where the foreground is fast and the background is "very nearly stopped."

Nowhere does the specification state what this device **does for a user**. There is no requirement naming a target workload the first release must serve, no floor on the roster, no acceptance criterion of the form *this configuration runs this set of things at this quality*. [userspace-porting.md](userspace-porting.md) is the closest thing and declines the job in its own words: the applications are "the **elective** applications" and the roster is "elective and stageable," a sequencing document by design, so nothing on it can fail to arrive. [performance-estimates.md](performance-estimates.md) scores deltas against an out-of-order baseline and reports the honest matrix by archetype; it does not answer whether the resulting absolute is a product.

That omission is what makes *delete-rather-than-defend* structurally unfalsifiable. Every deletion is scored on the axis it improves (one fewer Sail obligation, one fewer block on the least-built arrow, one fewer trust anchor), and the ledger runs only that way, because the axis that would push back (does the surviving machine still do the job?) has no requirement to be violated and no artifact to run. Twenty-plus deletions are each booked as a win at specification time, and not one of them can be contradicted until silicon exists. A design method with no possible negative result is not being tested by its own process; it is being narrated by it. No register closes this one, because the missing artifact is a product floor and writing one is a decision rather than a composition.

The figures that price the decision are already published, and they are conditional in both directions. [performance-estimates.md](performance-estimates.md)'s headline total is roughly 30-55% of a conventional core on general interactive code, worse on browser workloads, with the accelerated paths at parity or far above; the vertical capacity lever is conditional on a materials result rather than graded by effort (R-15-163); and the first release carries the radio roster whole (R-18-004). "Mobile/laptop class" is therefore conditional and unfalsifiable as stated, and the missing artifact is a fork rather than a floor alone: either name this a fixed-capacity secure appliance, or state a measurable mobile acceptance criterion. Both are product decisions and neither is recorded. Nor is the fork a free choice between two open options: accepting authenticated external or bonded memory reopens R-15-162, which declines chiplets and bonded die-stacking outright, a deliberate refusal carrying a stated ground.

**5. The cache deletion depends on an unauthored physical premise, and the premise is now two constants rather than one.** Flat per-class access latency is what replaces DRAM's row-buffer variance and turns the worst-case memory-access term into a fixed constant (R-15-163, R-15-164, R-15-247), and *deterministic* does not imply *fast*. The magnitudes that decide whether either constant is livable are [crown-jewels.md](crown-jewels.md) row 15, *the timing-annotated Sail model's latency magnitudes*, status **not authored** (R-17-041, R-15-095, R-18-024): the table exists and carries a row per operation class, and not one figure in it is measured. The static hierarchy with fixed per-region latency classes that a poor result would otherwise want as its fallback is already the design rather than an escape from it: R-15-247 makes main memory two static latency classes under one placement discipline, assigned at composition by the memory plan that already assigns banks (R-08-012a, R-08-012e), each entering §11 as one architecturally visible constant, with no cache, no migration, no wake-on-access, and no promotion between them. So the escape route is spent, and the exposure widens in the same act: there are two constants to be unlivable rather than one, and the second class's four are additionally hostage to R-15-247m's qualification measurement of a repaired megabit-class macro that nobody has built (R-15-247n, R-15-247s).

## The theorem tier covers the hardware nobody has written

**6. Single-prover purity holds exactly where nothing exists, and is abandoned where artifacts do.** The Kami/Kôika Coq refinement is "the only unbounded vehicle" (R-17-039) and is authored **for the net-new blocks that have no legacy RTL**: the capability/tag-carrying DMA fabric, the TDM NoC, the fixed-function sequencers. Those blocks do not exist. The cores that *do* exist (CHERI-CVA6, Ara, Gemmini) close by riding "Sail-generated SystemVerilog plus commercial FEV," which R-17-039 concedes "trusts the equivalence tool," or by a deferred proof over shipped SystemVerilog conditioned on "**if** a foundational Verilog-semantics account matures."

The absence contract has the identical shape: Kôika blocks close in-prover as a predicate over the Coq term; imported cores close on "a structural **audit, not a theorem**." [absence-contract.md](absence-contract.md) states this plainly, and states that day one *is* the imported-core half, "which is exactly the half that closes on audit."

The composed statement is uncomfortable and is nowhere made: **the strongest claims this design makes about the processor it will actually run on rest on a commercial tool's say-so and a netlist search, while the Coq tier (the whole argument for the single-prover discipline) covers only hardware that has not been designed.** That is not dishonest; both halves are booked. It is unassembled, and assembled it says the trust story for the CPU is evidence-tier for the foreseeable future. The absence contract's day-one availability, which §18 celebrates, is availability of the *weaker* discharge form. R-01-002a names the evidence-tier vehicles where G2 is claimed, so the *isolation* claim carries its tier; the observation here, that the single-prover discipline's own tier covers only blocks nobody has designed, is stated in neither §17 nor the absence contract. The tier is carried by the claim that rides the hardware and not by the argument for the discipline that is supposed to underwrite it.

## Booked but open

**7. Duress books its own failure modes and not the dynamic that creates them.** R-17-054's third limit books the honest half: the erase is "a countermeasure rather than a guarantee," it protects future recoverability only, which concedes the adversary who images the device first and compels second, and it is irreversible on accidental entry. R-17-030h carries the seam, stating that the erase is irreversible on the wrong side of a human protocol this design does not model. What neither books is the dynamic a publicly specified duress feature creates: an adversary who knows the feature exists compels *both* credentials, or watches for the erase and escalates against the person rather than the device. The mechanism's real protection is that human protocol; the document names it as unmodeled without saying what its failure costs; and the erase is irreversible on exactly the side where the coercion continues. The entry is narrow and it is not closed, because overclaiming this feature endangers the user who relies on it most.

**8. Physical and sub-partition side channels grow more load-bearing as the device model grows more adversarial.** Probe-level power/EM beyond the masking theorems' model, TEMPEST coupling, RF fingerprinting against link-layer unlinkability, macro-internal and inter-tier coupling, and the event-driven sensor readout's data-dependent timing are all booked (R-17-003b, R-17-055, R-17-058/059) and none is closed. The *seized-and-once-unlocked* and *duress* cases put a physical adversary in scope **by design**, which raises the weight of exactly these residuals. R-17-059 states the sharpest version itself: "the design has **no defence-in-depth layer beneath the package boundary** on the memory path … and if it is ever judged wrong there is no second mechanism behind it." That is the dominant fact again, at the physical layer, stated by the document against itself, and unlike the isolation tier it has no ladder, because a package boundary admits no staged discharge form.

The second static latency class adds two residuals here rather than inheriting any, and both are worse in kind than what this section already carried. **R-17-058e is the sharper of the two**: the class's refresh is a read-and-rewrite of every cell running unconditionally and periodically over the whole array, so a power or near-field probe yields a recurring whole-memory Hamming-weight readout **with no access induced at all**, which is the only member of this class that needs neither software nor a request to provoke. Its reach is narrower than the array and the narrowing is real rather than a mitigation, model weights dominating bulk capacity and being public; what it bites on is the user-derived fraction, the KV cache above all. It is why R-15-189m's disclosure claim is stated of the rail rather than of a domain's current. **R-17-058f is the one with no construction behind it at all**: hours of power-off plaintext across hundreds of gigabytes where the first class gave seconds, with authority remanence discharged by R-15-247d/e/q and confidentiality remanence discharged by nothing. R-15-247q narrows the window to domains live at power loss and does not close it. Both are stated as accepted costs of the capacity rather than as open work, which is the honest form, and neither has the shape R-17-058a's construction has.

One residual is answered in the form the rest want: R-17-058a carries the masked crypto core, with *d*-probing and composition theorems verified on the artifact against a probing-model statement conferred as a crown jewel, per-operation randomness booked against the entropy root and the core's slot, and the entry reduced to the axiom the theorems rest on: a physical model of the silicon whose faithfulness is review-gated and characterized at bring-up rather than assumed. The `B-01`×`P-6` cell reads the same way, proved at the model and booked outside it. The ceiling is real and named: a masking proof against a model the die does not satisfy verifies perfectly and leaks, so the class's whole weight hangs on one conferred statement that is not yet authored, and none of the other residuals here has had that construction written for it; each still reads stronger in summary than in construction.

**9. The degraded-subset rate is budgeted and no requirement names the number.** R-17-035 splits containment (fast, monotone, no proof) from remediation (proof-gated) and budgets *the window*; the quantity in which repeated forcing of the degraded subset is visible is **windows per unit time**. R-03-007/008 put denial in scope and R-17-030m supplies the quantity: entries into containment are budgeted as a rate and recorded as an attested countable event class rather than absorbed as containment succeeding, and the `B-06`×`P-5` cell reads `proved / detected`, the honest mode for it. What is open is what the ceiling *is*. The obligation says the quantity must exist and be recorded, no requirement names a figure, and a well-posed budget with no figure in it is materially better than an unposed one and is not the same as a closed one.

## Alternatives evaluated and declined

*Scope for this section and the five that follow: [architectural-alternatives.md](architectural-alternatives.md) (AA) and [inspirations.md](inspirations.md) (IN), spot-checked against [spec.md](spec.md) and [requirements-register.md](requirements-register.md). AA and IN are non-normative by their own headers, and entries move between the two documents as the material settles, so subjects are named rather than attributed to a file.*

**ISA and microarchitecture.** Belt/Mill-class deferred to a hypothetical gen-2 (protection model already subsumed by CHERI; backless memory fails the admission test on allocate-time data-dependent timing; the spiller is inseparable from the belt), with EDGE/block-atomic preferred over the belt if the lever is ever taken. Itanium/EPIC/VLIW rejected as base direction: it abandons the RISC-V substrate for a post-mortem ecosystem, the hoped-for simplification runs backwards, and microarchitecture-coupled binaries fork the single-recompile-target premise. Secure speculation via information-flow tracking (SecureBOOM, STT, DOLMA) imports nothing: a foreign prover, maximal inflation of the least-built arrow, and it closes only one of four blockers. Non-speculative out-of-order and the invisible-speculation family are rejected on the design-specific ground that the latency wall OoO exists to hide is deleted on the first latency class and scheduled rather than shadowed on the second, with the position under a single-planar-tier outcome stated in the entry. SEAM-V decoupled vector backend, OISC and transport-triggered architectures, test-bit-and-branch, a bespoke base ISA, bespoke capability semantics, `Zcmt` table jumps, and self-timed datapath logic are each declined with a stated ground. Fixed-slot barrel multithreading is the one partially admitted entry, logged as the first-choice gen-2 non-speculative throughput lever, with dynamic-issue SMT rejected; the entry states the scope explicitly, the SMT ground reaching the dynamic-issue form and nothing wider.

**Execution and metadata substrates.** CHERI-Wasm as a hardware ISA rejected as ISA, sandbox target, and deployment format. ELF as the on-device executable and package format declined, most of its machinery serving excluded facilities and the residue leaving an attacker-facing offset-linked grammar inside the verified loader. High-level-language computer architectures rejected, the safety atom already being CHERI and the semantic-gap closure belonging to the verified compiler. Tile-state extensions (x86 ACE, Arm SME) not imported. Bespoke full-PQC and NTT instruction sets declined, RVV already giving straight-line constant-time lattice arithmetic. Mon CHÉRI conditional capabilities and an initialization tag plane rejected. Programmable tag-policy engines (PUMP, SAFE micro-policies, CoreGuard) rejected: the rule cache is hidden history-dependent state surviving a partition switch, and runtime-loadable policy inverts the frozen-with-the-proof discipline.

**OS structure.** Language-based isolation as the *sole* mechanism (Singularity, Verve, Tock, Midori, Theseus) rejected, one unsafe block or miscompilation getting unrestricted access. Six Oberon mechanisms declined individually. Exokernel and unikernel structure recorded as already converged rather than imported. Decentralized-information-flow architectures (HiStar, Asbestos, Flume) not imported as an architecture, DIFC already being present over a capability kernel and CHERI at finer granularity. MultiZone mapped component by component onto strictly stronger mechanisms, with the inverse proposal (M-mode plus PMP only, no CHERI) rejected as the base on four grounds. Historical capability-machine runtime machinery (KeyKOS banks, meters, keepers; orthogonal persistence; single-level store) declined, whole-machine persistence conflicting with measured boot and eager zeroize, and storage otherwise becoming a second origin of authority able to resurrect a capability after revocation. Object Memory Architecture not imported, the OID-to-location indirection being the deleted MMU generalized to per-object. Enclave architectures (Sanctum, MI6, Keystone, CHERI-TrEE, Tyche) not imported, defending against speculation and sharing this design already deletes.

**Devices and peripherals.** External roaming authenticators, DIVA, dedicated fixed-function GPUs, uniform graphics widening, Vulkan and software GPU personalities, panel-resident aging compensation, Adaptive-Sync, firmware-driven NPUs, physical bifurcation of the radio onto a second die, and FullMAC radio controllers are each declined. General lockstep and TMR are deployment-deferred rather than rejected, available as graded options where a safety case pays for them.

**Memory technology.** Memory encryption and the integrity tree declined outright, memory cryptography protecting an interface this machine does not have. SRAM chiplets and bonded die-stacking declined, each die being a separate mask set and supply-chain entity. Dynamic and adaptive low-leakage SRAM techniques, active sub-threshold memory, transparent variable-rate compression, and runtime deduplication declined. Non-volatile main memory and unified SOT-MRAM storage carries a full five-claim decomposition and a disposition: reject the wholesale all-CAAC-IGZO, all-SOT-MRAM single-level store as the base, keeping bespoke volatile SRAM normative. **Oxide-semiconductor 2T0C gain-cell decks belong to this section only as evidence**, being normative as the second static latency class (R-15-247); what the alternatives document holds is the device evidence and the four-construction reset analysis the adoption rests on. The 2T-SOT-MRAM candidate is rejected rather than deferred, on the ground that it ranks second within a class that has an occupant, so admitting it would mean displacing an adopted class rather than filling an empty slot. Static code overlays are deferred behind a measured trigger rather than rejected. Deleting the store buffer for sequential consistency by absence clears four of five gates and is booked as an open design-space question with an explicit falsifier.

## Rejections whose reasoning is premise-dependent

These are not errors. Each is a place where a disposition rests on something the documents themselves book as unfinished, and the disposition is stated with more finality than its premise supports.

**The single-prover axiom underwrites a whole family of rejections.** SecureBOOM/UPEC, GLIFT/SecVerilog, riscv-formal, EasyCrypt, HACL\*/libcrux, Verve, VeriBetrFS/Perennial, and above all the refusal to inherit seL4's Isabelle proof all turn on it. That two checkers in the trust base are strictly worse than one is a value judgment rather than a theorem, and proof diversity is the standard counter-position. The seL4 entry concedes the two-checker alternative's edge is narrower than it looks and that the inherited proofs would not cover this configuration anyway, which means the maturity argument does little work either way. Every foreign-prover rejection inherits the premise.

**Verify rather than hedge rests on a proof that does not yet exist**, which is the dominant fact above; what belongs here is the list of dispositions hostage to it, and the list is shorter than the five that cite it. The PMP-backstop, IOMMU, MTE, shadow-stack, and initialization-plane rejections all name CHERI as the mechanism the design verifies most deeply, while R-17-039 records the Sail-to-RTL layer as the least-built and notes no artifact at full-application-core scale. R-17-039a sorts them by what the arrow actually supplies: the PMP backstop and the shadow stack's backward edge are hostage outright, hedging one failure on the imported core the three-route ladder reaches last and standing meanwhile on evidence about the mechanism they were to hedge; the IOMMU's ground is the single address space and MTE's is the rule against a statistic, neither of which the arrow carries; and the initialization plane keeps a genuinely disjoint mechanism in eager zeroize, for the disclosure consequence alone. So the disjoint-failure-domain hedge is dropped on the strength of a future artifact for the first pair, R-17-037 books that concentration honestly, and what stays open here is that pair rather than the five.

**The out-of-order rejection's premise is two magnitudes nobody has measured.** It is no longer the vertical tier count. The entry states its ground per class, R-15-247 putting bulk behind a slower constant by design and R-15-247j keeping every §11 hard task and all hot code on the first, and it states its position under R-15-173a's single-planar-tier outcome, which moves capacity and neither constant. What the disposition still spends is the size of both, and finding 5 carries them as unauthored: the ground is that the first class's constant is small enough that an in-order core leaves little on the table, and that the second class's is larger but known ahead of the access and therefore schedulable rather than shadowable. The second half is structural and holds whatever the figure is; the first is an assertion about a magnitude the timing-annotated model does not yet carry, and *deterministic* does not imply *fast*.

**The memory-encryption rejection rests on a scope line the documents call load-bearing.** R-17-059 states it plainly: the memory path is defended by absence of surface, and if that line is ever judged wrong there is no encryption to slow an attacker and no freshness check to catch a replay. The claim that an attacker at that level would equally reach the keys is asserted rather than analyzed, and separate-key-holder constructions exist precisely to complicate it.

*The documents are internally disciplined about booking honest residuals, and most of the weaknesses above are acknowledged somewhere in the text. The charge is that the dispositions cite those premises as settled when arguing against alternatives.*

## Where the documents admit a simpler design suffices

The MultiZone entry is the bluntest: PMP-only's appeal is real and almost entirely about realization, and it is rejected as the base *on the goal function, not the effort function*. For a goal short of maximal assurance, the simpler design suffices, and the document says so. KataOS is used as a foil that stops an assurance tier short on every shared axis and actually shipped. GrapheneOS is recorded as the terminus of the hardening road on commodity hardware. Static code overlays concede that the architecture may reject an application whose resident image exceeds the fixed SRAM budget, an accepted capacity limit rather than an omission. The all-SRAM capacity ceiling states the density price plainly, leaving memory-hungry workloads to a DRAM design. Radiation hardening and lockstep are graded to the deployment. Barrel multithreading, deferred-fault poison loads, and wider superscalar are all deferred to gen-2 should the in-order tax bind, which is the same admission from the other side: if performance never binds the simpler base suffices, and if it does the base was over-simplified. Honest costs are booked for residual judder, no-GPU throughput, absent credential portability, no legacy radio coverage, and scalar cores without scratchpads.

## Redundancy and complexity the documents admit

**There is no duplicate revocation mechanism.** A derivation tree would be a second mechanism for a property the CHERI machinery delivers over strictly more of the machine, and R-08-004 carries the CHERI mechanism alone. IN records the argument rather than the duplication, which is the right residue: the exclusion is the load-bearing half of taking the CHERIoT-shaped object model.

**The store buffer meets four of five deletion gates and is retained pending operands that do not exist.** By the design's own logic it is a structure currently carried as a candidate for deletion; the fifth gate's condition is restated over named symbols and evaluates to unevaluable on every class, so what retains the buffer is the absence of the schedule, the binding map and the selected microarchitectural widths rather than a measurement anyone is waiting on a number from.

**Temporal safety is covered at three layers.** The static memory plan fixes placement, the CHERI-TAL linear and affine discipline covers compile time, and the load filter with its budgeted sweep and quarantine covers runtime. The documents frame this as composition, and R-05-159 states it as a conjunction rather than a hedge, but it remains the largest standing stack of overlapping mechanisms for one property.

**Two interface formalisms plus a label layer.** A WIT-derived type layer joined to a FIDL-derived wire layer, with flow labels as a first-class concern, is deliberate and is still real surface.

**Five retained instruments are kept on performance arguments alone.** Fractional LMUL, three VLENs, vector masking, indexed gather/scatter with segment operations, and hardware divide and remainder each fail the deletion gate on cycles rather than on proof benefit. The rejected-simplifications table records these as judgment calls.

**Three asymmetries a critic can name.** Macro-op fusion is admitted precisely because it is architecturally transparent while other transparent extensions are cut; the sentinel gets the only lockstepped core, since a detector cannot report its own corruption; the eUICC is the sole admitted foreign computer. Each is justified in place.

**Deferred machinery still looms.** Static code overlays and generation-tag temporal safety are both preserved as options, and each would retire a removal the absence contract enumerates: the `Zifencei` exclusion, whose ground is that no runtime write-then-execute consumer exists, together with the deleted on-device loader for the first; the second-metadata-plane exclusion for the second. Both are named in that register against the trigger that would fire them (R-15-100b, R-15-047, R-13-010, R-15-035), so the removals read as contingent on the capacity bets exactly where they are.

## Register: the conferral mechanism

Requirements carrying `· Fail-closed:` confer into R-17-030r's seam register, and those carrying `· RoT-fresh:` confer into R-10-013a's freshness enumeration, with both directions checked in each: an unconferred member and an uncollected conferral each fail the build. Finding 3 carries the assessment: conferral closes the collection's disagreement with the requirements and not completeness, the vocabulary sweep with forced disposition is the only backstop, and an author who rewords a refusal defeats it.

**The two registers take opposite directions, and each now says which and why.** R-17-030 grows by *addition*: a refusal is booked by writing a seam beside the others, because R-03-009 prices every member against availability alone and member by member, so what the collector adds is the composition and never the admission, and an author with a refusal to book owes a seam of their own rather than an amendment to somebody else's obligation. R-10-013 grows by *amendment* and gates its own conferrals: its sentence is a budget over a counter with a rated endurance rather than a roll-call of members, so a state admitted without reopening it is endurance spent by nobody, which is the quantity R-10-013d bounds and R-17-030q refuses at admission. The asymmetry is the design and not an oversight, because the same direction on both would either put a roll-call inside R-17-030, which R-17-016 forbids as a second statement of membership free to drift, or let the counter's budget grow one entry at a time with nobody reading the total.

**The forcing function is unequal between them, and was weakest exactly where the direction is strictest.** Nothing makes an author add either property line except the vocabulary sweep and the review gate, which both registers state. What neither stated is that R-10-013a's own rule ran on nothing: citation is not membership, so a conferral naming a state the enumeration does not carry satisfied every rule that read it, and the conferral count that would have disagreed is derived, so `--fix` rewrites the figure as the member lands and leaves the sentence forbidding it standing over its own falsification. K-73 closes that by holding the enumeration's states and its conferrals' states against each other in both directions, by the state's own words rather than by an id.

## Register: machinery cost

**Letter-suffix density is a signal worth reading.** Roughly a fifth of entries are post-hoc insertions. Prose-order numbering with permanent IDs is a deliberate and defensible choice, and this is not an argument against it; the density is evidence about edit rate against review stability, which is a different question the register does not ask.

**The coverage matrix is the heaviest single machinery piece.** A full bipartite product of boundaries against properties, one row each, is tool-checked and therefore consistent, but it manufactures a large derived artifact with perpetual maintenance cost, and its failure mode is a pair citing a requirement that does not actually carry it, which is precisely the fidelity gap the checker admits it does not close: finding 2's face three, landing on the heaviest artifact.

## Not gaps: the ceilings this document keeps naming

*These are stated correctly, they are permanent or nearly so, and numbering them as findings would pad this catalogue's own count in the way it charges the specification with inflating claims elsewhere.*

- **Specification error is first-ranked, and is the reason the inventory exists.** Twenty-six crown jewels, each a small oracle that must be *stated* right and whose wrongness no proof catches: the delimited-release bound, the ASN.1/UPER codec, the dedup keyed-digest interface, the HAL's DMA postconditions, the IDL initialization annotations, the WCET latency magnitudes, the `Zkt`/`Zvkt` leakage model, the Ztso and static-prediction fetch models, every wire-format mapping, each Lustre program. Unchanged in kind, large in extent, and measurable, which is the whole point of having counted them.
- **Fabrication trust is evidence-based, not proof-based.** IRIS backside inspection makes fab an obtainable-evidence question rather than an act of faith, and the tier grading is a genuinely strong position: everything that *acts* is imaged, and what is not imaged cannot act. The ceiling stands where R-17-063 puts it: coarser than the smallest features, *evidence not proof*, and a sub-resolution fab adversary stays in scope. Narrowed, not removed.
- **The bedrock assumptions, the human, and the eUICC stay outside the trusted computer.** MLWE/MSIS and ECDLP/CDH are irreducible axioms; the user is outside every consent theorem, on the comprehension half that stays irreducible (R-17-013) and the abuse-resistance half the register books open by name (R-17-013e); the eUICC remains "the one tolerated foreign computer," contained to zero platform authority but an admitted exception to the design's defining claim. All three are permanent, and the *no foreign computers / everything verified* framing should keep saying so out loud.
- **The booked residuals are booked, not overlooked.** Wrong responses to a failed verdict, runtime-value-dependent overflow, analog power and EM leakage, human consent, protocol-standard flaws, and fail-closed denial are each recorded as residuals rather than as overlooked bugs; where one earns a finding above (3, 8), it earns it for what the booking still lacks, not for being missing.
