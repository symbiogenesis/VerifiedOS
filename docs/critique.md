# Critique

*Non-normative. The canonical critique, in three movements: a gap catalogue for the current state of [spec.md](spec.md) and its companions; a review of what the dispositions in [architectural-alternatives.md](architectural-alternatives.md) and [inspirations.md](inspirations.md) rest on, which is not a second copy of the inventories those documents own; and a review of the register machinery itself. All of it is checked against the artifacts: the atomic-requirements register and the seven derived views. Where an entry here and the register disagree, the register wins and this document is defective.*

*References are symbolic: requirement IDs, crown-jewel rows, and section headings, never line numbers, per the register's **traces are bookmarks, not line numbers** rule; a line number is a derived fact restated in a second artifact with nothing checking it, and it goes stale on the next edit to the artifact it cites. Counted figures a document owns are either recomputed by `tools/check.py` from that artifact or stated loosely on purpose; a hand-restated exact figure is a derived fact with nothing checking it. A disposition, a lineage, or a status another document owns is cited here and never restated, for the same reason. When a finding closes or a work-list item lands, delete it, renumber where the numbering is positional, and retarget every cross-reference that pointed at it: no departure notes, no strikethroughs, no closed section; git history is the narrative, and what stands here is the open agenda. The same rule governs a simplification that is adopted or declined outright, and a partly adopted one keeps only the part still in question.*

*Two outside critiques sit at the repository root rather than under docs/, on the rule that routes a note outside the corpus away from the gate-audited tree: [GEMINI-CRITIQUE.md](../GEMINI-CRITIQUE.md) and [KIMI-CRITIQUE.md](../KIMI-CRITIQUE.md), each commissioned by the owner and each ending in a project-added section that no rule reads. The Gemini note's, *Checked against the corpus and not carried*, lists the figures it asserts that no artifact in the repository supports and the residuals it presents as unnoticed that the register books, so that a reader does not cite them back as findings; the Kimi note's, *What the project did with this, and what it left standing*, is a disposition table naming the entry each finding landed at and which findings stand. Neither is a corpus document, and neither is inventoried here.*

The review machinery makes the project's real position **countable**, and it is worse than the prose reads. Twenty-six crown-jewel specifications are named; **two** are authored. Ten theorem targets are named; **none** can start, because each is premised on a specification that does not exist. Every hedge the design deleted was deleted on the strength of a verified primary, and **no primary is verified**.

**Not one of the numbered findings below is a legibility problem.** A legibility gap closes by writing a paragraph, and no finding does. What does close by writing, or by a call somebody has to make, is not charged as a finding; it stands on the work list, priced by what closing it costs.

## The architecture's irreducible compromises

*This section is judgment rather than a second findings ledger. Not every unattractive trade is a defect, and a project may remain the wrong product even if every theorem it names is proved. The point is to separate necessary ugliness, limitations of the present verification toolchain, and risks the design's own accounting underweights.*

### The three objections

**The two-class memory system is a rational defeat, and still a defeat.**
The conceptually clean machine has one uniform latch-based memory, with 6T cells where density matters and higher-transistor-count cells wherever stability, voltage margin, or read isolation matters more.
That machine has one access contract, one retention story, one power-off story, and no placement decision whose meaning depends on which kind of cell holds an address.
It also cannot supply the capacity this project's workload floor asks for, local models above all, inside a reticle-limited device.

R-15-247 answers with oxide-semiconductor 2T0C gain-cell decks for bulk storage.
The answer is defensible, but it gives back much of the conceptual compression the all-SRAM design bought: a second fixed latency, a retention deadline, scheduled refresh and discharge, bank-current and power-delivery constraints, a second power-off confidentiality story, and a placement rule with no promotion or migration escape hatch.
Worse, the second class is normative before R-15-247m's repaired-macro qualification exists and before the [bank-count exploration](bank-count-dse-contract.md) has the physical coefficients that prune or rank a candidate.
The higher-transistor-count all-SRAM machine remains the prettier architecture; the gain-cell machine is the capacity compromise, not an equally elegant refinement.

**The executable golden model does not become the product merely because it runs.**
On the hardware side the golden artifact is a C emulator generated from Sail, while the product is synthesized RTL that must be shown to refine the Sail term.
On the software side the host oracle is Gallina lowered through CertiCoq to Wasm, while the device artifact is GC-free purecap machine code produced through CompCert-C, Fiat/Bedrock synthesis, Vélus, or the arena route.
The [implementation plan](implementation-checklist.md) does preserve one important identity: once produced, the same purecap device binary runs on the emulator, the RTL in co-simulation, and the FPGA.
But that binary is not the host oracle, and the Sail emulator is not the RTL.

This is less a bad local decision than an honest limit of the field.
No verification language in this stack is simultaneously the most convenient specification notation, a directly synthesizable whole-machine implementation, and a producer of excellent optimized production binaries.
The project therefore pays for compiler correctness, lowering, assembly, linking, image construction, RTL refinement, and the semantic bridges between them.
Calling the sources singular does not make those correspondence obligations disappear.

**Reuse is broad in the bibliography and narrow in the shipped artifact.**
The project does pull in many third-party projects, but predominantly as pinned readings, executable references, differential oracles, proof lineages, or designs to re-home in Coq.
Very little can be lifted unchanged into the final machine because the platform rejects the POSIX/Linux contract, standard privilege and memory machinery, ordinary executable loading, runtime allocation, firmware controllers, and any load-bearing proof checked outside the selected kernel.
The custom capability dialect, typed IDL profile, package format, kernel, memory system, and admission language then narrow the reusable set again.

Most individual refusals are defensible.
Their compound cost is still an ecosystem island: less hardware choice, fewer mature drivers and libraries, less upstream testing on the exact configuration, and more code for which this project is the first serious integrator.
Standards buy more than compatibility.
They buy independent implementers, adversarial interoperability, accumulated failure knowledge, test corpora, replacement suppliers, and a second interpretation of prose that can disagree with the first.
The design is right to reject a standard whose mechanism violates a security invariant, but it should count the loss of that epistemic diversity every time, not only the loss of applications that happen to speak the interface.

### Unknown unknowns and underweighted consequences

**A model that fits in memory may still be unusable for inference.**
The dense class is justified most visibly by local language models, but capacity is not their binding performance quantity here.
R-15-247p and R-12-085 make the second-class bank grant set the admitted token rate, and the performance account states that inference is bandwidth-bound while the matrix array's compute multiple acts on a term that is already slack.
At low batch size, generation repeatedly streams much of the resident weight set; fitting the weights proves neither useful tokens per second nor acceptable joules per token.
Until the qualified macro supplies sustained bandwidth, bank activation current, bitline energy, retention work, and thermal behavior together, the flagship justification for the second class remains a capacity result in search of a product result.

**Private inference and efficient sparse inference pull in opposite directions, and the design resolves it by scope rather than by mechanism.**
R-15-171 admits a mixture-of-experts model only with every expert resident and fixed top-k, making the amount of work per token constant while routing still selects expert addresses.
R-05-070 rejects any secret-labeled value that reaches a memory address, so the two coexist only because R-12-085 keeps session content outside the secret label: a prompt carries its client's confidentiality label and never the constant-time one, the server is not a secret-touching compartment, and a client holding secret-labeled material has no typed channel to it.
The same scoping admits variable context lengths, token-dependent sampling, early exits, sparse attention, and paged KV-cache techniques on the same footing and no other.
What it costs is stated in two places: the routing's data dependence is confined to the server's partition by the schedule and rides the near-field residual R-17-058e books, and a secrecy class that wanted those techniques constant-time gets no local inference at all.
The workload used to justify dense memory is thus also the workload whose modern efficiency tricks the constant-time policy could never admit, which is why they are kept outside it.

**Static perfection spends utilization twice.**
The offline memory plan does excellent work: it colors non-overlapping live ranges, charges a compartment on the peak of its aggregate live set rather than the sum of separate peaks, and makes exhaustion local and explicit.
It cannot pool across an island boundary, and R-15-172 still sizes an island to its peak rather than the machine to the sum of averages.
The [occam/transputer precedent](inspirations.md#occam-perihelion-helios-the-transputer-and-xmos-xcore-static-channels-in-silicon-and-the-boundary-every-rendezvous-machine-stops-at) sharpens the name: the planner really does delete classic allocator external fragmentation, so this is not the transputer's dynamic-heap "Swiss cheese" recurring under another spelling (R-08-012).
What remains is **reservation fragmentation**: an empty pre-colored slot or pool member is physically idle yet unusable by a compartment the static plan did not assign it to, and a bank bound to another island cannot be borrowed at all (R-08-012c, R-08-047).
The machine's aggregate idle capacity is therefore a sum of residues that need not be spendable by the workload peaking now.
A conventional virtual-memory system combines address indirection with machine-wide physical-page allocation, allowing a page no longer backing one address space to back another; the advantage is statistical multiplexing across owners, not a better packing algorithm.
This machine deliberately forgoes that general free-page pool (R-08-045).
The cyclic executive makes the same trade in time: R-07-036 burns an idle slot across confidentiality labels rather than donating it.
Population rungs, same-label rotation, suspension, and declared shedding soften the result, but no mechanism lends an idle bank, pool member, or slot across the boundary that made it safe.
The product therefore pays for peak isolation in both bytes and cycles, so effective capacity and throughput can fall together on bursty interactive workloads.

**Proof simplicity can become a proxy for physical truth.**
R-15-108 correctly makes proof simplicity an objective of design-space exploration, because a smaller state machine is cheaper to refine and easier to audit.
The Goodhart risk is that a compact formal model can be obtained by pushing difficult behavior below the model boundary: a memory access becomes one fixed constant, while process variation, retention, droop, yield, and temperature become qualification assumptions.
The second memory class is the clearest instance, adopted partly because carrying one architecture with two constants is formally smaller than carrying a branch over whether the class exists.
A design that is easy to state is not thereby easy to fabricate, and a constant in Sail can be the name of an unsolved analog system rather than a simplification of one.

**A small implementation trust base can still have a large conceptual trust base.**
One Coq kernel and one Sail anchor minimize the code that may grant a theorem, which is a real strength.
They also concentrate common-mode error: an omitted observation, a wrong capability rule, a weak non-interference statement, or a mistaken physical abstraction can be shared by every proof above it.
The review gate and the independent QEMU, RTL, parser, and protocol oracles are the right responses, but those oracles are deliberately evidence rather than authority.
They can reveal disagreement and cannot make agreement evidence that the shared abstraction is the right one.
The project's smallest trusted *implementation* therefore depends on a much larger trusted act of judgment: choosing the right semantics and leakage model in the first place.

**Energy is a weaker gate than correctness, despite being existential for the reference product.**
The architecture moves codec, graphics, radio, sensor, and inference work onto general-purpose verified cores to delete firmware trust bases.
It also burns schedule slack, keeps large memory populations at declared retention states, performs background refresh, ECC scrub, and revocation reclamation, and gives up the energy efficiency of mature fixed-function media blocks.
Some of those costs are individually small and several are offset by static power states, but they compose inside a mobile or laptop thermal envelope.
The critique below already observes that no scheduled artifact can falsify the design in joules.
A system can close every logical theorem and still fail as a portable computer because the battery, skin temperature, acoustic cooling budget, or sustained clock cannot carry the proof-friendly realization.

**The memory bet is a yield and economics bet as well as a device-physics bet.**
A repaired test macro can establish cell behavior and still say little about the yield of a reticle-scale logic die carrying many memory decks, native capability tags, stronger tag ECC, repair structures, power delivery, and tight cross-tier alignment.
Repair must preserve the fixed-latency contract and the island binding rather than merely return the nominal bit count.
If usable yield or test time is poor, the architecture can be functionally correct and commercially impossible, with no external module or commodity memory vendor available to absorb that risk.

**Evolution remains proof-gated even where initial construction is modular.**
A component whose implementation changes under a stable contract can often be re-certified locally, and the design has a fast containment path while the full fix is proved.
A change to a semantic anchor, capability encoding, protocol grammar, memory map, leakage model, or shared interface reopens the seams above it; support for a new radio generation or memory technology may also require new silicon.
This is the security benefit of freezing the machine and the operational cost of doing so.
The risk is not merely slow feature delivery: it is a long degraded interval after the outside world changes in a way the frozen assumptions did not anticipate.

**The largest unknown unknown is correlation.**
The bespoke memory, capability dialect, compiler path, static kernel, scheduling model, package format, and server ecosystem are often justified independently.
They are not independent bets.
Most depend on the same premises: workloads can be bounded ahead of time, fixed latency is affordable, offline composition can replace adaptation, and enough optimized code can be recovered without dynamic machinery.
If one premise fails at product scale, several layers can fail together: the memory plan no longer fits, the schedule no longer admits, the compiler cannot hide the latency, and compatibility offers no substitute implementation.
The project is strongest when it deletes one mechanism after another from a proven base; it is weakest where several novel mechanisms lean on the same unmeasured physical or workload assumption.

### Overall assessment

The architecture is unusually coherent, and many of its bespoke choices are defensible when judged one at a time against the stated goal of minimizing trust.
The criticism is that local defensibility does not establish global practicality.
The programme most needs early falsifiers rather than further obligations: a qualified dense-memory macro exercised at the bandwidth and energy point local inference needs, a production-path vertical slice connecting the executable specifications to real device binaries and RTL, and independent implementations that attack the semantic anchors rather than merely agree with them.
Until those exist, the design's conceptual beauty is real, but it is the beauty of a conditional construction whose hardest conditions are physical, economic, and ecological rather than logical.

## The dominant fact, which is not a finding

**Every hedge is spent, no primary is verified, and the interval between is claimed at the tier it holds.** This is stated where it belongs, so it is not a defect and not an entry. It sits ahead of the findings because it is the single most important true thing about the project's assurance position, and because several of the findings below are read against it.

The *verify rather than hedge* rule is applied consistently and its logic is sound at the fixed point: a mechanism whose only job is to catch the failure of a formally verified primary buys little against the cost of a second modeled mechanism. On that rule the design deletes the MMU, PMP, the S/U rings, the IOMMU, the initialization-tag plane, MTE, shadow stacks, the Harvard split, and every disjoint backstop on the machine (R-17-037, R-17-045). §17 states the hedge that remains: "the **formal verification of CHERI itself**," resting on "the **RTL ⊑ Sail** arrow … and its Coq-native restatement for the CHERI-RISC-V profile as the residual." That arrow is the layer the same document calls **the least-built layer of the stack** (R-17-039, R-18-010). The CHERI-TAL soundness metatheorem that replaces the initialization-tag plane is theorem target `CJ-TAL-SOUND`, premised on the ISA profile and **not started**. The certifying compiler, the on-device checker, the Cerise universal contract, the secure-compilation theorem: none exists.

So the rule is sound at the fixed point and its premise is false everywhere before it. On the entire trajectory to that fixed point (a multi-year interval on this roster, and the only state the project has ever occupied) the primaries are *unverified*, a hedge against an unverified primary is ordinary defense in depth, and any device that executes in that interval runs with the elegance and without the argument. A single CHERI logic error in monotonicity, revocation, or the tag plane fails every isolation boundary on the machine at once, with nothing beneath it and, until `CJ-TAL-SOUND` and the Coq refinement close, no proof above it.

**The interim claim is the knob that turns, and it is the only one that can.** Demanding retained backstops asks for the wrong thing: MMU, PMP, the S/U rings, the IOMMU, MTE, and the Harvard split are **silicon**, so "retain until the theorem closes" means fabricating them, modelling them in Sail, and paying the second-modeled-mechanism cost the rule exists to price, and R-18-002 forecloses a capability-degraded interim by name. What can move is what the interval claims for itself, and §1 carries that on G2 (R-01-002a, R-01-002b): the software premise admits no interval, since nothing runs uncertified and a missing certificate costs delivery rather than admission; the hardware premise stands at **evidence tier** (rvfi, Sail-generated SystemVerilog under commercial FEV, the imported-core netlist audit) rather than **theorem tier** (the Kami/Kôika Coq refinement); every FPGA and early-silicon unit asserts G2 at that tier and no higher; the tier rises per core when that core's unbounded refinement is admitted, never at an intermediate rung; and an attestation or datasheet asserting G2 without its tier is a review-gate finding. §3 carries the inheritance (R-03-006). The retirement condition is R-18-010's rungs, on R-05-022's two-list discipline.

**What the tier statement buys is legibility, not safety, and the exposure is untouched.** The interval is multi-year and the tier is the weaker one for all of it; what a reader of §1 and §3 alone learns is that this is so. Two consequences run into the findings below. Whether a future datasheet honours R-01-002b is a semantic question `check.py` cannot reach, which is finding 2. And the tier claim is load-bearing for exactly as long as the interval lasts, which makes it the easiest thing in this catalogue to let go stale.

## What is not built

**1. Nothing load-bearing is built, and the specification is precise enough to say how little.** [crown-jewels.md](crown-jewels.md) is the instrument that says so, and its status column reads: of twenty-six crown-jewel specifications, **two are authored** (the frozen ISA profile and the apex theorem's machine-checked statement), five are partial, **nineteen are not authored**. The ten theorem targets each depend on a row above; nineteen of those premises do not exist, so most of the proof program cannot begin rather than merely being unfinished.

The repository's own ledger sharpens this, and what it sharpens is *which kind* of artifact exists rather than how much of it there is. Some seven weeks, seven-hundred-odd commits, and three-and-a-half megabytes of design documents stand against a tree whose non-Markdown paths are, by size: the curated Sail model, vendored from its upstream pin with a project-authored layer over it (the dialect's custom encodings, the platform instructions, the frozen Keccak unit, the core-class and memory-class tables, the timing annotations); the consistency checker, and the assembler that stands in for a toolchain nobody has re-homed yet; a differential corpus of two dozen hand-written purecap programs that assemble to ELF and run to a HTIF verdict on the emulator generated from that model; the Gallina statement artifacts under [proofs/](../proofs/), which are the apex theorem's machine-checked statement with its assumption gate, the seam witnesses beside it, and a dozen-odd further files in the same idiom, each stating one surface the plan's Gallina lanes schedule, which the section below reads; and a score of pinned upstream submodules, which are start-froms rather than authored code. **Every one of those is a specification, an instrument, or evidence about one, and not one is the system.** No kernel, no firmware, no init, no filesystem, no crypto core, no userland, and no compiler backend: what runs on this machine today is a test harness proving that the machine's *description* is self-consistent. The loose figures are deliberate: every exact count in this catalogue sits under a `check.py` claim, and no claim holds these.

The project's only demonstrated velocity is in producing obligations. That is a defensible way to spend an early phase, and §18 and R-01-003 are candid that the as-existing assurance is low. The obligation count grows monotonically and the discharge count is two: every hardening that makes the design safer adds a crown jewel, and nothing retires one except authoring it. The inventory carries that direction as a **burn-down** with the implementation plan's milestones as its retirement mechanism, so what is open is whether the discharge count moves.

### What a statement artifact is, and is not

**The Gallina files are specifications stated in a prover, and a green proof gate over them is a claim about their form and not about the machine.** [proofs/](../proofs/) holds over a dozen `.v` files, tens of thousands of lines, landed item by item under the implementation plan: the cyclic executive and its partition context, the RoT discharge sequence, the M-mode and RoT firmware obligations, the composition-time admission path, endpoint IPC, the journal and index with the keyspace domains above them, the supervision tree, the memory plan, the generated ring contract, the handler graph, the Keccak reference, and the apex statement with its seam witnesses. Each transcribes what a register section obliges into definitions and theorems over a record whose fields are the quantities a composition fixes, and most say in their headers, in one shared sentence, what the gate's green line means: compiled, axiom-free, non-vacuous and enumerated, and it does not mean verified. [run.py proofs](../tools/vos/cli/proofs.py) decides exactly that line, every shipped constant's assumption set against the declared set R-05-164 reads from the register, which is empty until an admission axiom, the bootstrap root or an *Ax* ledger entry is authored.

What independent readings of the tree establish, stated loosely because none of these counts is this document's to own:

- **Every file is axiom-free and closes on itself.** Nothing is admitted, no top-level parameter stands, and a `Require` names a sibling in the same directory or nothing at all, so no theorem reaches the Sail model, an upstream, or a library beyond the prelude. That is the R-05-163 gate holding, and it is also the boundary of what the tree can say: a theorem over a record the file defines constrains that record, and the model in [model/](../model/) is not a term any of these files can mention.
- **The witnesses inhabit the statements and do not describe the machine.** Where a file instantiates a machine's carrier types to show its statement is inhabited, it instantiates them at unit, bool or nat. That is what non-vacuous means at this gate: a witness of that shape says the statement is satisfiable, and says nothing about whether the product satisfies it.
- **A refutation rules out a phrasing, not a system.** The constructions the files refute, a detector that searches the graph, a partition switch that drops a tag, a loader discovered at runtime, a rho recurrence started one step early, are alternative readings of a register clause, and refusing each one shows the clause as stated is not dead. None is an alternative design, and no refutation is evidence that the stated clause is the right one; that question is R-17-016's, and no proof reaches it.
- **One file constrains an object outside the tree.** The Keccak reference is checked against the standard's published known answers and held against the Sail unit's by K-91, so its theorems can be false of something that exists. Every other file's theorems are true or false of definitions the same file makes.
- **The apex statement is stated and nothing discharges it.** Its Vocabulary record is the coverage checklist, and every row of the [field-binding map](field-bindings.md) reads `none yet`: no proof workstream instantiates any field, so every seam is named and not one is closed.
- **No rule binds a theorem to an entry.** The requirement and `CJ-` identifiers inside the files are comments. K-42 holds the apex record's field list against its view, K-89 holds the ring artifact against its generator, K-91 holds the Keccak answers against the Sail unit's, and no rule reads any theorem as the discharge of any register entry; which statement transcribes which requirement is a reading a person makes and each item's completion note records, and a reading is what no rule makes.

So the tree moves the count of what exists on the specification side, and moves it in the currency of an authored statement rather than an authored proof: where a crown-jewel row is carried in part by one of these files, the [inventory](crown-jewels.md) says so and holds the row at `partial` until the property the row names is proved of the object it names. What the tree does not move is the dominant fact. No theorem in it is about the machine, no file in it is a program the machine runs, and each file's fidelity to the register it transcribes is held by a reader and by nothing the gate audits, which is finding 2's residue meeting the newest artifacts in the repository.

## The review gate audits its own reflection

**2. The gate is referential and the risk is semantic.** §5's release gate exists because "a proof against a wrong spec verifies perfectly," and R-18-034 states plainly that the gate "audits the register rather than this prose," so an unverified extraction stands between the reviewer and the design of record and is declared the authoritative object. That an automated checker cannot decide whether a statement is the *right* statement is a property of checkers, not a repairable defect, and charging it as a gap would be a category error. What is chargeable is the residue, in three pieces.

**The instrument is strong and the wrong shape.** `check.py` checks **referential integrity**: cited bookmarks resolve, requirements carry traces, no view drops a requirement in its bearing subsections, no asserted count disagrees with its artifact, no document carries encoding damage. Its reach includes this document: every count asserted here is anchored in the checker's registry and recomputed, so an edition of this critique cannot quietly disagree with the artifacts about a number. A requirement can nonetheless resolve perfectly, trace perfectly, appear in every view, count correctly, and state the wrong thing.

**Face one: part of the review budget is spent on the document rather than the machine, and the class wants a tag.** A class of acceptance criteria is discharged by inspecting the prose rather than the artifact: *"the axiom is stated in §6 and §17, not implied"* (R-06-014); *"the consent TCB is named in §6 rather than hidden in userland"* (R-06-018); *"the cost is booked rather than omitted"* (R-12-020). How large the class is depends on where the line is drawn, so the honest figure is a band with its rule attached: counting every criterion whose test uses *stated*, *booked*, *named*, *recorded*, or *declared* while citing neither `check.py` nor a companion artifact yields **157 of 1389 acceptance criteria**; requiring the criterion to cite a `§` as the thing a reviewer opens narrows it to **31**. Both are snapshots and lower bounds, since amendments keep adding entries of exactly this class. These entries are **not** defects under R-05-153: that rule catches a claim that cannot be restated as an atomic, testable requirement, and "the consent TCB is named in §6" is atomic and testable by inspection, which is a test. The defect is one layer over, in R-05-152 making the register the audited object: a criterion that is a fact about the commentary spends the gate's scarcest resource, a reviewer's attention on the one pass that decides whether the specification says the right thing, on editorial hygiene. The repair is a **tag rather than a defect entry**: mark the spec-hygiene entries as such, so the review can price them instead of discovering them one at a time.

**The amendment rate is the same finding from the other side.** The git log is the measure: repairs routinely land within a day of the finding that names them. That velocity is the strongest evidence in the repository that the specification workstream is real, and it is the problem: a corpus amended faster than anyone re-reads it is a corpus whose review is always of a previous edition.

**Face two: the extraction-defect list opens on the wrong axis, and its own stated class is empty.** The list is empty, and the register is right that "an empty list is not the same as a swept one." But look at what the list is instrumented to catch. Its findings arrive from building a derived view and machine-checking it, which reports an enumeration two documents disagree about, never a passage that would not decompose, because nothing re-reads the prose against that question. **R-05-153's actual class, a normative claim that would not decompose, has zero entries**: not one of the 1389 requirements has yet been booked as standing for a passage that resisted restatement, and face one's entries are not the missing ones, because they decomposed fine, into requirements about the document. So two readings stand. Either the prose was atomic throughout, implausible for a document whose §17 entries routinely run five clauses deep with three parenthetical qualifications, or the extraction **accommodated** the prose where it should have confronted it. The register does not claim otherwise: its standing instruction is that sweep 2's question "has not been asked exhaustively, so further instances should be assumed present rather than absent." The channel that reports what the *prose* refused to decompose is not running, and an agenda drawn from enumerations alone means the meeting R-18-035 calls the review gate's agenda has barely started.

**Face three: the derived views can be wrong in the half nothing checks, and there are more of them every month.** [crown-jewels.md](crown-jewels.md) is declared "Normative as a **view**." The *quantitative* half of that class is closed the only way such things close: `check.py` parses the status column into its three classes, recomputes the ratio, and fails where a document asserts a figure the artifact does not bear. The *qualitative* half, which is most of it, is untouched: whether a row's `partial` is doing work `not authored` should do, whether a cited requirement is the *right* ground, whether a discharge mode in [coverage-matrix.md](coverage-matrix.md) reports what its cited requirements actually carry, invisible by construction wherever the claim names no place. Every artifact that arrives widens that surface, machine-checked for **presence** and none for **truth**, and a view is normative whether or not it has been read. The only lever this finding has is substitution: *whether R-17-030a's seams are the right ones* is not answerable, but it is **replaced** by a mechanical question it implies, whether every requirement using the vocabulary of refusal is conferred or dispositioned (finding 3). The matrix is where that lever's reach is easiest to read and hardest to extend: one of its discharge modes, `residual`, names §17 rather than a construction, so a cell reporting its remainder as booked there is decidable against the section it names, while the rest of the vocabulary names constructions and stays a reading. That substitution is available wherever a qualitative claim has a lexical shadow it can be held against, and most of the list above has none.

**3. A membership claim is decidable against its own members and undecidable against the world.** R-17-016 closes the crown-jewel list by conferral: membership is asserted by each requirement that has it, [crown-jewels.md](crown-jewels.md) is the one place the conferrals are collected, and `check.py` fails on a conferral with no row or a row with no requirement. R-17-030r and R-10-013a carry the identical rule: a requirement specifying a mechanism whose failure action is to stop records the refusal against itself on a `· Fail-closed:` property line, a requirement placing state under the monotonic counter records it on a `· RoT-fresh:` line, the R-17-030 seams and R-10-013 collect them, and the checker fails on a conferral no collection carries and on a seam standing under no conferral. Thirty-five conferrals against nineteen seams, four against one enumeration, both directions decidable. What no checker can grep for is not membership; it is the *predicate*, and conferral does not need to.

**Conferral closes completeness for none of the three sets, and the registers say so.** A specification that ought to confer crown-jewel status and does not is exactly as invisible as a refusal that ought to confer and does not; what conferral closes is the *collection's disagreement with the requirements*, which is the smaller property. Each register claims the decidable half in its own text and declines the other, which is the only honest form of the claim.

**The machinery is not the result, and a completeness convention fails exactly where it is most expensive.** Against the completeness residue the checker carries the only instrument available to it: the vocabulary of refusal is over-approximated across every requirement body, and each candidate it catches must confer, be collected, or be dispositioned by name with a reason. The scan earns its keep: the seam register carries four members the enumeration alone missed, and one of them is the sharpest member in it. **Every fault detector on this machine answers detection by stopping** (uncorrectable ECC, tag integrity, the control-flow signature on the three protected sequences, the sentinel's lockstep comparator, residual synchronizer failure), each of them right to stop, and the adversary who provokes them is the radiated-EMI and electromagnetic-fault-injection adversary §3 admits by name and §15 shields against. The other three members are the entropy health test, whose sole failure action is fail-stop and whose meeting is every operation needing fresh randomness; the display path, whose visible blank is a caught fail-stop on the surface the consent prompt is drawn on, reaching denial of the prompt from a direction the compositor seam does not cover; and budget admission refusal, which also costs R-17-030e its claim to be the only member whose denial cannot reach a running unit.

**What stands is the residue.** The vocabulary agenda is lexical, proves no totality, and is defeated by an author who rewords rather than decides; the dispositions are judgments recorded in a tool; neither is a quantifier over the semantic predicate, because there is none to build. ## Not gaps: the ceilings this document keeps naming

*These are stated correctly, they are permanent or nearly so, and numbering them as findings would pad this catalogue's own count in the way it charges the specification with inflating claims elsewhere.*

- **Specification error is first-ranked, and is the reason the inventory exists.** Twenty-six crown jewels, each a small oracle that must be *stated* right and whose wrongness no proof catches: the delimited-release bound, the ASN.1/UPER codec, the dedup keyed-digest interface, the HAL's DMA postconditions, the IDL initialization annotations, the WCET latency magnitudes, the `Zkt`/`Zvkt` leakage model, the Ztso and static-prediction fetch models, every wire-format mapping, each Lustre program. Unchanged in kind, large in extent, and measurable, which is the whole point of having counted them.
- **Fabrication trust is evidence-based, not proof-based.** IRIS backside inspection makes fab an obtainable-evidence question rather than an act of faith, and the tier grading is a genuinely strong position: everything that *acts* is imaged, and what is not imaged cannot act. The ceiling stands where R-17-063 puts it: coarser than the smallest features, *evidence not proof*, and a sub-resolution fab adversary stays in scope. Narrowed, not removed.
- **The bedrock assumptions, the human, and the eUICC stay outside the trusted computer.** MLWE/MSIS and ECDLP/CDH are irreducible axioms; the user is outside every consent theorem, on the comprehension half that stays irreducible (R-17-013) and the abuse-resistance half the register books open by name (R-17-013e); the eUICC remains "the one tolerated foreign computer," contained to zero platform authority but an admitted exception to the design's defining claim. All three are permanent, and the *no foreign computers / everything verified* framing should keep saying so out loud.
- **The booked residuals are booked, not overlooked.** Wrong responses to a failed verdict, runtime-value-dependent overflow, analog power and EM leakage, human consent, protocol-standard flaws, and fail-closed denial are each recorded as residuals rather than as overlooked bugs; where one earns a finding above (3, 8), it earns it for what the booking still lacks, not for being missing.
